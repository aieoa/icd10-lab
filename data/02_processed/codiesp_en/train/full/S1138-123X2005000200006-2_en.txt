Patient with bilateral cleft lip and palate. The premaxilla was protruded and rotated to the right, and the lateral segments had collapsed. This protrusion made lip surgery difficult because it increased soft tissue tension.

After taking impressions, an ECPR was designed and placed under general anesthesia. The parents activated the device at home to achieve expansion and once a week we modified the tension of the chains to achieve retrusion of the premaxilla.

After six weeks, the premaxilla had retruded and the segments were aligned, so cheiloplasty could be performed with the Mulliken technique.
