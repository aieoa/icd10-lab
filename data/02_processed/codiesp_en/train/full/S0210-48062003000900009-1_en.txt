A 75-year-old woman with a history of obstructive pulmonary disease, high blood pressure, and hypercholesterolemia.
Consult our service due to the accidental ultrasound finding of a kidney lesion.
Ultrasound: complex, multiseptated cystic lesion, with polylobulated contours, 11 cm in diameter and with peripheral punctate calcifications, which occupies practically the entire kidney.
T.C. abdomino-pelvic: left renal lesion measuring 11 x 7 cm in diameter, multiseptate and with punctate calcifications. Absence of retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy.

The differential diagnosis between multilocular cystic nephroma, as a first option, and cystic renal cell carcinoma is proposed.
We performed isotopic renogram with Tc-99m. Left kidney with inferior uptake and functionality, with a cold central image that could correspond to an occupying lesion. R.I. function: 25%.
Bone scan: No alterations.
With the diagnostic guidance of Bosniak III kidney injury, a decision was made to undergo surgical intervention, performing a nephrectomy.
Pathological report: Multilocular cystic nephroma, without atypia.
