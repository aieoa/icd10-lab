45-year-old male, worker in quarries for 15 years. Diagnosed with complicated silicosis in FMP. Ex-smoker for two years, 25 packs/year. Pulmonary tuberculosis in 2007. He attended the Emergency Department in March 2009 with threatening hemoptysis, preceded by cough and brownish sputum for 4 weeks. The physical examination revealed normal vital signs and bilateral rhonchi on lung auscultation. Serum analysis revealed leukocytosis (11,280 cells/μl with 80% neutrophils) without other alterations. Fiberoptic bronchoscopy revealed a large clot in the right upper lobe bronchus. The CT scan showed bilateral and diffuse micronodular interstitial involvement, some calcified nodules, predominantly in the upper lobes and conglomerates that had increased compared to previous studies, with the appearance of material inside, as well as nodular and diffuse pleural thickening. The culture was positive for Aspergillus fumigatus in sputum and bronchial aspirate. In blood, IgG precipitins were positive and the galactomannan antigen was negative. Embolization of the bronchial arteries of the LSI was performed with control of hemoptysis and treatment with voriconazole was instituted with good evolution after one year of treatment with negativization of serum precipitins.
