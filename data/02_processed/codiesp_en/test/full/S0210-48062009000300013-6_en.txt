58 year old woman. Calcium calyceal lithiasis of the left kidney.
Personal history: Severe HTN that was difficult to control, since on a previous occasion Leoch had to be suspended until the blood pressure levels were stabilized with study and treatment by the Nephrology service. Moderate obesity.
First Leoch. No. and intensity of waves: 4,500 to 4.
Clinic: Admitted two days after left kidney Leoch with left lumboabdominal pain and hypovolemic shock with Hb of 7 and Hct 24.
Four units of packed red blood cells were transfused over the days of admission to stabilize Hb between 9 and 9.5.
A CT scan confirmed the existence of a subcapsular hematoma with stone fragments and left pleural effusion.

In IVU a few days later, there was functional delay of the left kidney and poor visualization of the calyces and infundibulum due to their compression with a normal ureter.

Thoracentesis was performed with evacuation of 200 cc of blood fluid.
Twelve days after the hematoma occurred, it was drained by lumbotomy; Four days later she was discharged in good general condition. Subsequent radiological and analytical controls confirmed normal bilateral renal function.
