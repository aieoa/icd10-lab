42-year-old woman with a personal history of umbilical hernia surgery, HTN. She presented pain in FII of 2 years' duration, rectal tenesmus and tapered stools. Her physical examination revealed diffuse pain on deep palpation in FII. In the analysis she showed 16,000 leukocytes and 91% neutrophils. An opaque enema and colonoscopy were performed, both of which revealed a stenosing image at the sigma level. The histological study of the biopsies was inconclusive. Resection of the stenosing area was performed with end-to-end anastomosis. The histological study confirmed the lesion of colon endometriosis with transmural involvement causing areas of fibrosis and hemorrhage.
