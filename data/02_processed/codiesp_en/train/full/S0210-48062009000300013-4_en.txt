34-year-old male patient.
Personal History: Hepatitis in childhood. Right ureterolithectomy 10 years earlier. Cystinuria. IVU prior to Leoch with normal bilateral renal function but with a right kidney smaller than the left. Not HTA.
Leoch of 1 cm lithiasis in the lower calyx of the right kidney. 3,500 impacts, intensity 3. 5-6 hours after treatment, he presented intense pain in the right flank, diagnosed with a discrete subcapsular hematoma by ultrasound. On CT three days later, 12 cm hematoma. Two packed red blood cells were transfused.

14 days after lithotripsy hematoma drainage with open surgery. Discharged from hospital 7 days later. Subsequent ultrasound and IVU controls are similar to those prior to Leoch.
