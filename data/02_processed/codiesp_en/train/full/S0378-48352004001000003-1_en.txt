A 69-year-old man with a history of stage D2 prostate adenocarcinoma with multiple bone metastases who had received chemotherapy treatment with mitoxontrone and prednisone a month and a half ago with clinical and analytical response. His personal history included pulmonary hydotid cyst surgery 50 years ago that required transfusion of blood products, left iliofemoral bypass in 1991 due to chronic arterial ischemia, and a 3-cm abdominal aortic aneurysm in follow-up.
He was admitted with symptoms of asthenia, anorexia, pruritus and mucocutaneous jaundice, with choluria and acholia for two weeks. His physical examination revealed mucocutaneous jaundice, hepatomegaly about 23 cm from the right costal margin, painful on deep palpation without peritonism.
Blood test showed: Hb 13.5, leukocytes 3,900 (lymphocytes 53%), platelets 159,000, prothrombin activity 53%, INR 1.58, fibrinogen 221, cephalin time 42.9, urea 38, creatinine 0.8 , glucose 110, total proteins 6.3, GOT 904, GPT 990, total bilirubin 10.7 (direct 7.2), GGT 399, alkaline foshtase 349, LDH 905. HIV negative serology. IgM HAV negative, HBsAg+, antiHBs-, lgM anticore+, HBeAg+ and antiHBe-. Negative HCV markers. HBV DNA positive.

Abdominal ultrasound showed no relevant findings except a normal-sized liver with diffusely increased echogenicity without focal lesions, vascular permeability, or signs of portal hypertension.
A transjugular liver biopsy was performed without complications and the diagnosis was acute hepatitis of viral etiology, with regenerative changes and slight portal fibrosis. There were areas of hepatocyte collapse and bridging necrosis.
Given the high probability of fulminant liver failure due to his underlying disease, it was decided to administer adefovir dipivoxil for compassionate use to control acute viral hepatitis. After the start of treatment, the patient experienced a favorable clinical evolution with progressive normalization of liver function. HBV DNA was negative two months after the start of treatment.
After 3 months of treatment, the control analysis showed a total bilirubin of 1.1, GOT 36, GPT 25, GGT 72 FA 426, and second-line chemotherapy treatment with docetaxel could be started due to elevated PSA and reappearance of spinal pain. dorsal and right shoulder.
