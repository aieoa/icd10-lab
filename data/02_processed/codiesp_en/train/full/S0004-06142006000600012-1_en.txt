A 62-year-old patient was consulted due to pain in the right hemiscrotum for three months. Physical examination revealed grade IV right varicocele, and a voluminous mass was also palpated in the right renal area. An abdominal ultrasound was performed, which revealed a 10 cm heterogeneous mass. approximately in the right kidney, left kidney without pathological findings. It is complemented by computed axial tomography of the abdomen and pelvis with intravenous contrast, where a heterogeneous right renal formation measuring 110 x 120 mm in diameter is evident; no retroperitoneal lymph nodes are observed. Difficult evaluation of the right renal pedicle.

A magnetic resonance imaging was requested that confirmed the right heterogeneous renal formation in the upper pole of 12 cm. of larger diameter, without involvement of the renal vein or the inferior vena cava.

A right radical nephrectomy is performed. Good evolution, discharged on the fifth postoperative day.
The pathological anatomy reveals: Undifferentiated malignant tumor of the lineage to be differentiated by immunohistochemistry (IHC). Tumor size 13 cm. Local invasion: the tumor extends through the renal capsule, causing focal infiltration of the perisuprarenal fat. Renal hilum free of injury. Vascular embolisms present, surgical margins free of tumor lesion. Non-neoplastic kidney: chronic pyelonephritis. Artery and arteriolonephrosclerosis and 4 cm retention cyst. larger diameter. Adrenal gland free of injury. IHC:WT1 +++,CD 117 +++,CD56 +++,P53 -.

Diagnosis: Nephroblastoma (Wilms' Tumor), stage II of the NWTS classification.
The CT scan one month postoperatively shows tumor recurrence at the level of the nephrectomy bed.
He started chemotherapy regimen with vincristine, dactinomycin and doxorubicin. During the course of chemotherapy, he presented with pneumonia with deterioration of his general condition, sepsis and months after the nephrectomy.
