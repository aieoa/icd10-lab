Female patient, 62 years old, obese, with diabetes and hypertension for one year. For the past 6 months she has been reporting sporadic, low-intensity right lumbar pain. The physical check was normal. An abdominal ultrasound was performed, which showed a hypoechoic right adrenal mass measuring 80 mm that compressed the upper pole of the right kidney. Descending urogram, expansive process in the right adrenal, the upper pole of the right kidney displaced downwards. In the helical CT, tumor mass with a greater diameter of 8.7x76 cm with irregular contours, heterogeneous density of 100 - 37 HU, septated, compatible with an adrenal tumor of fatty density. The hormonal study to determine cortisol was normal.
The patient underwent surgery through a lumbar approach. The intraoperative findings were a tumor located in the right adrenal gland, well delimited with irregular borders with lobulations. Right adrenalectomy was performed without complications. The patient's postoperative evolution is favorable, remaining asymptomatic.
The pathologist reported the piece with a weight of 171.4 g, dimensions of 9 x 8 x 6 cm and the cut with yellow-orange areas and other areas of hemorrhages and adipose tissue alternating with areas of calcifications. Histopathological examination: myelolipoma of the adrenal gland with extensive areas of calcifications.
