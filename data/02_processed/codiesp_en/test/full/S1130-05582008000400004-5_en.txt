This patient, daughter of patient #3, was the subject of a biochemical and radiological screening study at the age of 14 years. She was found to have hypocalcemia, hyperparathyroidism, two maxillary tumors (from the second and fourth quadrants) and several renal cysts.
A right parathyroidectomy was performed to treat a parathyroid adenoma. Calcemia remained normal for a year, but from then on it began to increase. The patient underwent further surgery (left parathyroidectomy, both superior and inferior, and excision of half of the right superior parathyroid due to an adenoma of the left superior parathyroid). The maxillary lesions continued to grow and a few months later the two ossifying fibromas were resected.

She subsequently had another episode of hyperparathyroidism and she underwent surgery to resect the adenoma from the remaining gland. There is no indication of recurrence or the presentation of new maxillary tumors.
