A five-year-old male presented with perianal erythema accompanied by anal pain during defecation, afebrile. Anal and pharyngeal swabs are collected and a rapid test is performed to detect streptococcus, which is positive in the anal, with GABHS growing in the rectal culture. Treatment with oral penicillin is started. After 20 days he returned because, despite initially improving, the erythema had reappeared with less intensity accompanied by constipation and fissures on examination. Topical mupirocin was prescribed with partial improvement of the condition after four days, associating topical clotrimazole due to suspicion of fungal superinfection, with final resolution of the condition.
