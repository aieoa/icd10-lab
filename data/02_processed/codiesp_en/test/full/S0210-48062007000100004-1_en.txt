A 23-year-old man diagnosed with hyperreflexic bladder secondary to myelomeningocele. Carrier of ventriculo-peritoneal shunt due to hydrocephalus. Despite medical treatment, the patient presented symptoms of total urinary incontinence, with the need to use a penile collector, as well as symptoms of repetitive pyelonephritis predominantly on the right side. The cystography showed the presence of a low-capacity, scalloped contour bladder with passive grade III right vesicoureteral reflux and active grade I left vesicoureteral reflux. The cystography data were confirmed by the urodynamic study.
Laparoscopic extension enterocystoplasty was performed with a 15 cm detubularized ileal segment. The duration of the surgery was 6 hours. The bleeding was about 100 cc. The postoperative period was uneventful and the patient was discharged from the hospital on the ninth postoperative day.
After 18 months of follow-up, the patient is continent and has had no new episodes of pyelonephritis. Cystography performed 6 months after surgery revealed the disappearance of right passive and left active reflux. Only the persistence of right grade I active reflux was evident.
