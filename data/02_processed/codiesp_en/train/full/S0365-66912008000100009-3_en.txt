43-year-old woman with LE pain and photophobia. History of Scopinaro surgery one year ago, following a postoperative period with anemia, varicose veins in the lower extremities, hypocalcemia, and seronegative gonarthritis.
The LE examination revealed a marginal abscess, diffuse epitheliopathy, and conjunctival keratinization. Corrected VA of 0.6 in RE and 0.3 in LE. After being resolved with cyclopentolate (Ciclopléjico®, AlconCusí, S.A., Barcelona, ​​Spain) and a topical quinolone (Chibroxín®, Thea S.A., Barcelona, ​​Spain), it was noted that the epitheliopathy did not improve despite the lubricant treatment (Viscofresh® 0.5% , Allergan S.A.) maintaining a VA of 6/10 in OD and 5/10 in LE. Oral pilocarpine (Salagen®, Novartis SA, Barcelona, ​​Spain) 5 mg every 6 hours was added, without modification of the Schirmer test (2-3 mm in OD and 5 mm in LE). When conjunctival keratinization and diffuse epitheliopathy persisted, 20% autologous serum was added. In three weeks, the tear film increased (Schirmer 5 mm in OD and 7 mm in OI), regressing conjunctival hyperkeratinization, with a final VA of 8/10 in OI.
