A 46-year-old woman, with a history of ovarian endometriosis, who underwent an abdominal hysterectomy with double adnexectomy for endometriosis 45 days before attending the consultation, was referred by her gynecologist for presenting continuous pain in the iliac and sacral region since the immediate postoperative period. right that improved with anti-inflammatories and a discrete renal ectasia was observed in an abdominal ultrasound. On examination, the patient presented pain and guarding on deep palpation of the right lumbar fossa and tenderness on percussion and palpation of the left lumbar fossa, suggestive of retroperitoneal pathology.
Simultaneous intravenous urography (IVU) and computed tomography (CT) were performed, observing a functioning right kidney with delay in the uptake and elimination of contrast and erasure of the right psoas line. The CT scan showed a lobulated image of liquid content and apparent extravasation of contrast, with the ureter included in said formation. With the diagnosis of urinoma by ligation or section of the right ureter and after preoperative study, ureterolysis and excision of the urinoma are performed via the iliac route, with end-to-end anastomosis of the ureter after refreshing the edges and releasing both ends. A double J catheter is left guiding the ureter, which is removed one month after surgery. The patient's evolution has been favorable with no postoperative complications. In the control one year after the intervention, good kidney function was observed with normal urinary tract in IVU.
