72-year-old male, history: ex-smoker (for 17 years), moderate drinker and COPD under treatment; referred from the Digestive Service for study of dysphagia to solids and liquids with anorexia and progressive weight loss.
An oral endoscopy study was carried out, reporting the existence of a mamelonated and stenosing mass from 32 cm to the esophagogastric junction, in which a fistulous orifice was observed, with the path and its distal end unknown; In the biopsy, only the moderately differentiated, ulcerated and infiltrating epidermoid component of the neoplasm was found. The thoraco-abdominal CT scan shows a thickening of the esophageal wall in the lower third and esophageal-gastric junction, in the caudal portion of which a small filiform lumen remains, without identifying the fistulous tract described; without mediastinal or abdominal organ involvement. The barium esophagogastroduodenal study reveals strictures of 1-5 cm in length in the lower third of the esophagus, without other relevant findings. Ultrasound endoscopy shows a lesion that affects all layers of the esophageal wall that begins immediately at the subcarinal level, extending toward the left edge at which point it surpasses the adventitia, minimally penetrating the periesophageal fat; contacting the aorta and right pleura, without invading it; From the lymph node point of view, only a small subcarinal adenopathy with an inflammatory appearance was observed (T3N0).

An esophagectomy, intrathoracic gastroplasty and feeding jejunostomy were performed.
The pathological study of the surgical specimen showed a 5 cm infiltrating cancer with a well-differentiated and microcytic epidermoid component, with 2 isolated lymphadenopathy with a clear predominance of the microcytic component and involvement of the circumferential edge.
He subsequently underwent adjuvant chemotherapy treatment (carboplatin, etoposide) and radiotherapy (60 Gy). Ten months after diagnosis, the CT scan was repeated, which showed images compatible with liver metastases, which made it necessary to start palliative chemotherapy with etoposide, which he currently continues to receive.
