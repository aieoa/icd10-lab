A 13-year-old girl, diagnosed in May 2003 with intermediate uveitis and treated with topical, peribulbar and oral glucocorticoids, with persistent cystic macular edema (CME) in both eyes and visual acuity (VA) of 0.2 in the right eye ( OD) and 0.4 in the left eye (LE). In February 2004, 4 mg of intravitreal triamcinolone (Trigon Depot®, Bristol-Myers Squibb) was injected into her RE. One month later, the CME had resolved with a VA of 0.7, so treatment was repeated in the LE with a VA of 0.7. One year later, intraocular pressure (IOP) of 26 mmHg OD and 23 mmHg OI were detected and were controlled with topical timolol 0.5% (Timoftol® 0.5%, MSD). In July 2005, she had developed posterior subcapsular cataracts, with VA of 0.2 OD and 0.5 OI. She is currently awaiting phacoextraction surgery.
