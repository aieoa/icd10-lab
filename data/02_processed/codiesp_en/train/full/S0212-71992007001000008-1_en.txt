This is a 19-year-old man who was admitted reporting symptoms consisting of intermittent pain located at the level of the left hemothorax accompanied by cough and low-grade fever for one month. In the general analysis carried out, only the existence of a leukocytosis of 16,500 x 109/l and an ESR of 24 mm in 1 hour caught our attention; the rest of the complementary tests, including microbiological tests, were negative. In the chest in intimate contact with cardiac structures of 10 x 6 cm in diameter. The biopsy obtained by video thoracoscopy of the mass showed that it was constituted by a proliferation of Langerhans cells with abundant eosinophils. The histochemical stains performed showed positivity for S-100 and CD1a, being negative for lymphoid markers. All of these findings were compatible with the diagnosis. of HCL. After receiving 2 lines of chemotherapy treatment and without obtaining an obvious response, it was necessary to start radiotherapy, during which the appearance of a lymphadenopathy in a supraclavicular location was found, which after being biopsied revealed Hodgkin's disease type nodular sclerosis associated with LCH; After no response was observed to conventional treatment, autotransplantation of peripheral blood hematopoietic precursors was performed, achieving complete remission of both diseases to date.
