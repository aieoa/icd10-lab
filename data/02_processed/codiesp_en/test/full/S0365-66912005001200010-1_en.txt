A 30-year-old male with legal blindness since childhood, affiliated with ONCE, who comes to the consultation diagnosed with an intraocular tumor. The history included hearing loss; there was no evidence of neurofibromatosis. He had undergone surgery for cataracts in both eyes and glaucoma in the left eye (LE) 10 years ago. During the ophthalmological examination, visual acuity was between fingers in the right eye (RE) and amaurosis in the LE. He had pendular nystagmus. The study of the anterior segment highlights an iris coloboma in the RE, pseudophakia, and pupillary seclusion and the filtration bleb from the previous trabeculectomy in the LE. The intraocular pressure was 14 in the RE and 45 mm Hg in the LE. In the fundus, a coloboma of the retina and optic nerve (ON) was observed in the RE; It was not possible to visualize the fundus in the LE; the ultrasound showed retinal detachment and the presence of a mass in the lower temporal region. An MRI was requested and was reported as an increase in the diameter of the OD eyeball 32 x 26 mm and dilation of the NO exit; In the LE it had a size of 23 x 22, and a polypoid lesion was detected in the lower temporal part of the globe that was isointense on T1 and hypointense on T2 with enhancement after contrast, which could be related to amelanotic melanoma, associated with a slight thickening of the covers; In addition, an extraocular lesion at the exit of the ON was hyperintense cystic in T2 sequence without contrast enhancement.
With suspicion of an intraocular and ON tumor, enucleation was performed, which required lateral orbitotomy. Microscopically, an eyeball was observed that had a grayish tumor of soft consistency and NO thickening at one of its poles. The pathological study describes a proliferation inside the globe and under the retina formed by elongated cells with ovoid nuclei and fibrous stroma with thick-walled vessels, abundant glial fibers, Rosenthal fibers and microscystic areas. There was also an image of similar morphology with cystic changes and calcifications in the optic fillet. Immunohistochemistry was positive for S100, neuronal-specific enolase, vimentin and GFAP, Ki67 was positive in a low proportion.
