A 27-year-old male attended an Internal Medicine consultation referred by his primary care physician for: asthenia of three months' duration, nonspecific dizziness, and intermittent epigastric pain of moderate intensity. The relevant personal history is: allergic rhinoconjunctivitis. His usual treatment consists of ebastine 10 mg taken once daily during the seasonal allergy period.
The examination revealed marked paleness of the skin and mucous membranes. The analysis highlights hemoglobin of 6.5 g/dL (13-18 g/dL), mean corpuscular volume of 125 fl (80-100 fl), leukocytes of 6,000/mL (4,000-10,500/mL), platelets of 280,000. /mm3 (150,000-400,000/mm3, total bilirubin 1.35 mg/dL (0.3-1.2 mg/dL), aspartate aminotransferase (AST) 140 IU/L (4-50 IU/L), alanine aminotransferase (ALT) of 410 UI/L (5-47 UI UI/L), lactate dehydrogenase (LDH of 4,186 UI/L (140-240 UI/L). Before this analysis, a blood smear is requested that shows anisocytosis of macrocytic tendency, hypersegmentation of neutrophils and some dacryocytes. The study of iron and folic acid levels were normal, with serum vitamin B12 levels of 62 pg/mL (180880 pg/mL and homocysteine ​​of 32 μmol (4-15 μmol /L). Gastrin levels are clearly elevated at 539 pg/mL (normal < 100 pg/mL). Treatment is started with intramuscular injections of 1,000 μg/day of vitamin B12 for 7 days, then weekly for a month and maintenance therapy with one injection per month.
The blood count normalizes in the fourth week of treatment.
