67-year-old woman, native of Bulgaria, hypertensive, diabetic, obese, dyslipidemic and with chronic kidney disease (CKD) possibly secondary to diabetes and/or nephroangiosclerosis, on HD since January 2008. Low socioeconomic background, living with animals and poor hygiene staff.
A left brachiocephalic arteriovenous fistula (AVF) was performed, with a torpid evolution, performing HD due to transient CVC (multiple removals and new cannulations due to catheter entry port [PEC] infections).
She was admitted with fever and chills after dialysis, compatible with bacteremia, with blood cultures positive for Staphylococcus (St.) aureus. She associates infection in PEC, so the catheter is removed, which is cultured, growing AX, along with St. aureus and Enterococcus faecalis. With combined treatment for the three germs, the infectious condition disappears clinically and bacteriologically.
