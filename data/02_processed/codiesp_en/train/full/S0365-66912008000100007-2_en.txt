Girl, 11 years and 10 months old. Normal female karyotype, normal gestation and delivery. Brain magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) shows agenesis of the corpus callosum along with extensive abnormalities of neuronal migration. This patient presented epilepsy in childhood, with the first episodes of seizures at 2 years of age. Electroencephalographic (EEG) recordings demonstrate an asymmetric hypsarrhythmia. Funduscopic examination revealed a complete papilla in the right eye, peripapillary pigment, and areas of peripapillary hypopigmented chorioretinal lacunae extending into the retinal vascular arcades. However, there is no macular involvement. The left eye fundus presents a colobomatous optic disc and small chorioretinal hypopigmented areas. She has no other malformations at the renal or vertebral level. The patient is characterized by her long survival, with a current age of 11 years and 10 months, but presenting a very severe psychomotor retardation.
