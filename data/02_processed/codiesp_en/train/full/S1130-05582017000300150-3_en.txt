Radiolucent lesion in the ramus and part of the right mandibular body, involving the condyle and coronoid process in a 9-year-old male patient. Biopsy result, mandibular ameloblastoma. Given the extent of the injury, it was decided to perform a right hemimandibulectomy, with placement of a reconstruction prosthesis that includes the condyle. Control x-ray two years after the surgical procedure, where spontaneous hemimandibular bone regeneration is observed, the bone taking the same shape as the mandibular branch, and following the contour of a titanium metal plate. To date there is no follow-up on the case.
