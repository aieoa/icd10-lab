The first case is that of a 9-year-old girl who reported blurred vision. Her visual acuity was 0.2 and 0.3 according to Snellen optotype, respectively. Her refraction was determined under cycloplegia to rule out refractive errors, being + 0.75 in both eyes. The rest of the ophthalmological examination was normal, with a stereopsis of 60"-40". Upon further investigation of the anamnesis, it was found out that she was a girl adopted at 7 years of age. While she was drawing in the office, it was observed that she managed herself without difficulty, even dilated, so the symptoms seemed disproportionate and simulation was suspected. She resorted to the mirror test, turning out to see the 0.4 line with both eyes. Considering that plane mirrors have the property of doubling distances, its real acuity would correspond to 0.8. The case was discussed with the parents and it was decided to carry out periodic reviews. Her acuity improved to unity with both eyes at 3 months.
