55-year-old patient receiving cadaveric transplant. Induction: daclizumab-tacrolimus-MMF-steroids. Maintenance: tacrolimus-MMF-steroids. Creatinine 1 and 1.2 mg/dl; Creatinine clearance: 96 ml, without proteinuria. Nine months after transplantation, he presented viruria (107 copies/ml) for BKV, without renal dysfunction, with negative viremia. High viruria persists with negative viremia, which becomes positive at month 12 (> 104 copies/ml), the biopsy confirms interstitial nephritis, tubular nuclear alterations linked to BKV. The immunosuppressive medication was rotated to sirolimus 2 mg/day and cipro-floxacin 500 mg/day was added (10 days). He maintains stable renal function values, with clearance of 86 ml, without proteinuria and with a decrease in the viral load in urine and blood. Currently the patient preserves his kidney and did not require dialysis.
