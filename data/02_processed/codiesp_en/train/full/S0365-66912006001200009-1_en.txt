A 58-year-old patient with no relevant family history and a personal history of pharmacologically controlled arterial hypertension for 6 years and maxillary sinusitis of 10 days' duration without specific treatment. She went to the emergency room due to "loss and distortion of vision" in the left eye (LE) that had been going on for several days. The maximum visual acuity in the right eye (RE) was 1 and in the LE it was 0.125, extrinsic and intrinsic ocular motility were normal, and the slit lamp study showed no pathology. The funduscopic examination in the RE was normal but in the LE there was a macular serous detachment that extended to the superior and inferior temporal vessels, the optic nerve and the rest of the retinal parenchyma did not present alterations. She reported a cat scratch on the thigh of her left leg two weeks before the loss of vision. Seven days after being examined by us, she developed painful erythematous lesions limited to the lower extremities compatible with erythema nodosum that ulcerated within 20-25 days. She consulted the internal medicine service for a shared study. The chest x-ray was normal, the paranasal sinuses x-ray determined a slight clouding in the left maxillary sinus. Serological studies: ANA, ANCA, antiphospholipid antibodies were normal. CMV, EBV, Rickettsiae, Toxoplasma, HIV, Lyme and Luetic serology were negative. The Mantoux was also negative. Hemogram: Leukocytes 20,670, ESR 64 mm 1st hour, CRP 16.4 mg/L. Serological levels of IgM against B. Henselae by ELISA were positive at a titer of 1:812. The patient was treated with ciprofloxacin 1 gram per day for 14 days. Four weeks after treatment, the systemic symptoms began to return and the ophthalmological symptoms resolved, with the LE achieving visual acuity of unity.
