This is a 28-year-old patient, with no history of interest, who came to the urology clinic for a testicular nodule that he detected three months ago and which, according to his account, has not increased in size. Upon examination, a hard nodule, mobile and painful on palpation, was observed at the level of the right spermatic cord. The determination of tumor markers and hormones are within normal limits.
In the imaging study, ultrasound, an oval, hypoechoic image with mixed echogenicity is observed, which has a maximum longitudinal axis of 2.8 cm and which appears to be in relation to the spermatic cord. In the MRI study, a low signal T2 image is described at the level of the right spermatic cord, which indicates that it is a tissue with low water content, possibly fibrous or scarred, with a maximum diameter of 43 mm.

With the diagnosis of right paratesticular tumor, it was surgically excised, separating the tumor from the vas deferens without difficulty. The patient's course is uneventful and is currently asymptomatic.
In the macroscopic study of the piece, a rounded formation of brownish color and elastic consistency is described, well delimited, with a maximum diameter of 5 cm, and when cut it presents a whitish and homogeneous surface with a fibrous appearance. In the microscopic study, it is a well-circumscribed lesion made up of cells with extensive eosinophilic cytoplasm with the presence of transverse striations in some of them, immersed in a fibroblastic stroma, with occasional cells with a vacuolated appearance and multinucleated cells, with no mitotic activity observed. In the immunohistochemical study, intense and generalized expression for desmin and vimentin, and focal expression for specific smooth muscle actin, was observed in the neoplastic cells (Master Diagnostic. Granada. Spain).
