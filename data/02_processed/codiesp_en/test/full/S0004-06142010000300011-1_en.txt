A 24-year-old patient with a twin brother who in December 2005 underwent surgery for a combined neoplasia (mucinous adenocarcinoma and goblet cell carcinoid, T4N2M1) that infiltrated the appendicular wall and the meso, with multiple peritoneal implants and involvement of 8 of the 29 lymph nodes. isolated. He underwent right hemicolectomy, peritonectomy, and resection of macroscopic visceral peritoneum implants. Subsequently, adjuvant chemotherapy treatment was started with the mFOLFOX4 regimen for 12 cycles, with episodes of diarrhea after the second and third cycle and a hurticariform reaction after the 7th cycle. At the beginning of chemotherapy, 5HIAA in 24-hour urine was within normal parameters and body scanning with In.111 pentreotide and SPECT showed no alterations.
Ten months after surgery she presented with acute testicular pain. On physical examination, she presented a horizontalized right testis, enlarged in size and with a hard and irregular consistency in the upper pole, as well as thickening of the spermatic cord. In the testicular Doppler ultrasound, the right testicle showed increased caliber of the vascular structures, thickened epididymis, hypoechoic and with peripheral vascularization. Anechoic scrotal content with mobile echogenic content was observed, compatible with complicated hydrocele (pyocele and hematocele...). An urgent surgical exploration was decided, in which hematocele and thickening with induration of the vaginal testis were observed. Intraoperative pathology reported infiltration by mucinous adenocarcinoma. A right radical orchiectomy was performed with cord ligation at the root of the scrotum. The definitive pathological analysis showed a neoplasia of the vaginal testis made up of lakes of mucus and groups of epithelial cells, most of them with signet ring morphology, without involvement of the epididymis or the testicle.

After testicular surgery, chemotherapy with the FOLFIRI regimen plus BEVACIZUMAB was administered in the 3rd cycle. Nine months after the orchiectomy the patient presented hepatic, pancreatic and regional adenopathic progression, presenting subocclusive symptoms. He died 21 months after diagnosis.
