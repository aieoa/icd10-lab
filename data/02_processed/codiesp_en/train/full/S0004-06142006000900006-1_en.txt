A case is presented with complex stone pathology in which the stone sweeper was used as bilateral internal urinary diversion.
This is a 54-year-old male patient with 3 right renal lithiasis, the largest of which is 1.2 cm lodged in the lower calyx, who also presents moderate pyelocalyceal and ureteral dilation up to the junction with the iliac vessels, where ureteral lithiasis is located. 1.1 cm.; In this renal unit, a 'stone sweeper' catheter was placed prior to treatment with ESWL. On the left side, a 1 cm stone was observed in the lower pole, which was previously treated with ESWL, achieving partial fragmentation; In this renal unit, the 'stone sweeper' catheter was also placed prior to a second session of ESWL. The caliber of this catheter is 6.5 Fr and was maintained for a period of one month.

During the stay of the two 'stone sweeper' catheters, the patient did not report symptoms related to the lower or upper urinary tract, indicating good tolerance.
Their extraction was easy, without requiring anesthesia and small stone fragments were observed inside the baskets.
