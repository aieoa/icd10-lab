A 46-year-old man comes to the clinic due to viral conjunctivitis in the right eye (RE) of ten days' duration that has not improved with treatment with tobramycin and dexamethasone.
As a personal history, it is worth highlighting condylomas on the penis without urethritis six months ago and permissive sexual relations at present.
On ophthalmological examination, he presented visual acuity in the OD of light perception and projection. In the biomicroscopy of the anterior pole of RE, superior corneal thinning was observed with an imminent risk of ocular perforation, so the patient was treated with corneal coverage with multilayer amniotic membrane.

Given that the results of the conjunctival exudate culture are positive for Neisseria Gonorrhoeae, intravenous antibiotic treatment with ceftriaxone was started at a dose of 1g/12h and topical treatment with reinforced ceftriaxone eye drops (50 mg/ml) for 21 days.
One month later the patient had visual acuity in OD of 0.8; In anterior pole biomicroscopy, superior corneal fibrosis is observed with good transparency in the central part and a well-formed anterior chamber.
