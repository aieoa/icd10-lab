Male patient, 68 years old, former textile industry worker, sent to the clinic due to complex renal cystic mass. History of rheumatoid arthritis, controlled hypertension, dyslipidemia, pulmonary tuberculosis 40 years ago, medicated with indapamide, sinvastatin and NSAIDs. He denied smoking habits or exposure to chemical agents. He reported exposure to sheep in his youth. He denied any known history of kidney stones. Underwent left hip arthroplasty years ago. No history of abdominal or genitourinary surgery. For months he had been reporting the elimination of gelatinous material in the urine, without specific form, accompanied by episodes of total macroscopic hematuria, without clots. Without other accompanying symptoms, such as low back pain, nausea or vomiting, or low urinary complaints. Without anorexia, asthenia or weight loss. No respiratory or gastrointestinal symptoms.
On physical examination, there was swelling of the left flank/lumbar region, which was not painful. No hepatomegaly, no abnormal abdominal collateral circulation. Genitals without clinical varicocele. Lower limbs without edema. Without peripheral lymphadenopathy. Pulmonary ausculation without apparent alterations.
In analytical terms, he had slight anemia (Hb 12.7 g/dL), without leukocytosis. Relative eosinophilia (2800/µL). VS 67mm. Creatinine 1.2 mg/dL. Urea 77 mg/dL.
Carrier of an ultrasound revealing a right kidney of somewhat reduced dimensions (10.8 cm) and with increased echogenicity of the parenchyma, presenting a simple cyst in the upper pole. The left kidney presents a voluminous complex cyst of the upper pole (70 x 68 x 67 mm), presenting evident solid content and regions associated with posterior acoustic shadowing. Subjected to abdominopelvic CT - voluminous complex cystic formation that contained gross parietal and central calcifications, occupying the upper two thirds of the left kidney, as well as fine septa inside, presenting attenuation values ​​that varied between 10 and 20 Housefield units. After contrast administration, contrast uptake by the walls is evident. No lesions of abdominal solid organs, presence of retroperitoneal lymphadenopathy or morphological alteration of the adrenal glands were evident. Chest x-ray without significant alterations, such as cystic lesions or alterations compatible with lesions of a metastatic nature or active tuberculosis. Mantoux (tuberculin test) negative. Search for acid-fast bacilli negative. Urine cultures (multiple samples) in Lowenstein medium were negative. Serology for Echinococcos granulosis and multilocularis negative.

With the diagnosis of Bosniak class IV complex cystic mass and in the absence of support for lesions of a specific infectious/parasitic nature, he underwent left radical nephrectomy through a lumbotomy incision over the bed of the 12th rib.
The pathological examination showed a neoplasia measuring 11.5 cm in size, consisting of glands with a simple and complex structure, the epithelium being moderately differentiated and centered by mucoid material in the form of large "pools" of mucin with tumor fragments. The lesion invaded the renal parenchyma without affecting the capsule. The remaining parenchyma showed signs of pyelonephritis with slight atrophy. Surgical margins free of tumor. Conclusion: mucinous adenocarcinoma of the renal pelvis PT3NxMx.

After obtaining the histological result, he underwent upper digestive endoscopy and colonoscopy, which showed no alterations.
A 10-month follow-up was carried out. The patient is completely asymptomatic, with no evidence of disease recurrence, locally or remotely.
