A 42-year-old man presented with a right testicular mass and pain of 2 months' duration, which progressively increased. He denies any history of pathology or other symptoms. Testicular ultrasound showed diffuse increase in the size of the right testicle, which measures 6.2x5.8x4.2 cm, with diffuse alteration of its echogenicity. Therefore, he was taken to an orchidectomy and found an enlarged, rubbery, whitish testicle. The histopathological study revealed diffuse infiltration of the testicular parenchyma by a malignant hematolymphoid neoplasm composed of small, irregular cells with little cytoplasm, which presented angiotropism, with associated areas of necrosis. The tumor cells were positive for ACL and CD3 with Ki67 of 90% and negative for TdT, CD30, ALK, bcl2, bcl6 and CD10. With the above, the diagnosis of unspecified peripheral non-Hodgkin T lymphoma was made.
