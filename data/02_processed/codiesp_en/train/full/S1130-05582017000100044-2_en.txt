A 49-year-old man with a personal history of multiple paragangliomas. In 2005 he underwent surgery for the right jugular paraganglioma using an infratemporal approach with good post-surgical evolution. In 2014, surgical treatment of a right vagal paraganglioma was decided due to tumor progression, with paraganglioma excision performed via the laterocervical approach. In the postoperative period, the patient presented right facial paralysis grade II on the House-Brackmann scale, cophosis and paralysis of the right cranial nerves IX, X, XI and XII. In successive check-ups in the clinic, the patient presents dysphagia, dysphonia, and also reports very intense pain in the infra and preauricular region that appears with the beginning of chewing at all meals, and which progressively subsides. A decision was made to inject hyaluronic acid into the right vocal cord, inject botulinum toxin into the right cricopharyngeal muscle, and given the suspicion of PMS, it was decided to infiltrate botulinum toxin into the right parotid region. 5 units infiltrate 12 points, totaling 60 units. After 15 days, the patient noticed a notable improvement in the dysphonia and complete disappearance of the painful symptoms.
