47-year-old male, referred to us from the Rehabilitation Service in February 2011, for evaluation of his obesity. The patient had a personal history of a spinal cord injury after a work accident in 2004, with a vertebral fracture and incomplete paraplegia at the level of L1. He smokes more than 20 cigarettes a day and has high blood pressure that he treats with enalapril 20 / hydrochlorothiazide 12.5 every 24 hours and untreated hypertriglyceridemia.
In the guided anamnesis, he reported a lot of difficulty in his daily life (he requires a wheelchair to move around and uses standing for transfers). He admitted to having a big appetite and snacking between meals. Since 2006 he has tried several times to lose weight, without success. On examination, he weighed 135 kg and had a height of 1.72 cm, BMI 46.62 kg/m2. tA 145/92. In analysis he highlighted cholesterol 222 mg/dl and triglycerides 260 mg/dL with HDL cholesterol 41 mg/dl.
After being evaluated by a multidisciplinary team at our hospital, and contraindications for surgery were ruled out, his case was approved and he underwent a laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass in January 2014.
One month after surgery the patient feels well, he had no complications in the immediate postoperative period; and he currently denies vomiting. He has less appetite and takes a fractional liquid diet. His current weight is 120 kg. From the point of view of his cormobilities, he has stopped hypotensive treatment and BP control at home is optimal (110/90). Analytically, he has not presented a vitamin deficiency, with supplementation of a vitamin complex per day.
