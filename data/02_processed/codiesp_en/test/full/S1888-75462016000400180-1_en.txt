A 53-year-old male, veteran road cyclist, with no relevant medical-surgical history, toxic habits or known allergic reaction to medications, who consulted our emergency department for a fracture of the middle third of the right clavicle and a fracture of the right costal arches. produced in an accident with a fall on the road.
Upon admission, he presented in good general condition, being hemodynamically stable, without dyspnea, complaining of pain in the right clavicle and costal wall without symptoms or signs of neurovascular involvement of the right upper limb. The simple radiological study revealed the existence of a fracture of the fourth and fifth right costal arches without evidence of hemothorax or pneumothorax, and a fracture of the middle third of the right clavicle with a third fragment, confirmed in the computed axial tomography (a, 1b).

It was initially treated with analgesia, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs and placement of a figure-of-eight bandage, and the patient consulted three days later in the same department due to swelling of the right upper limb without loss of sensitivity and with preserved peripheral pulses, normal vital signs and cardiorespiratory auscultation with noise. normal heart sounds at a normal rate without murmurs or extratones, and preserved vesicular murmur in both lung fields, without rales or other pathological findings.
Spirometry, electrocardiogram, and echocardiogram were normal; The blood analysis showed abnormal values ​​of MCHC=31.4g/dl, neutrophils=76.6%, lymphocytes=16.4%, eosinophils=0.9%, fibrinogen=645mg/dl, GGT=52U/l and positive D-Dimer. The venous echo-Doppler study revealed venous thrombosis of the axillary, cephalic and basilic veins with compression of the ulnar branch of the medial brachial cutaneous nerve at the level of the basilic hiatus (a, 2b).

The figure-of-eight bandage was replaced with a simple sling that was maintained until the beginning of functional rehabilitation, and treatment with Tinzapain sodium was performed, with radiographs and serial control Doppler ultrasonography until the consolidation of the clavicle fracture and complete recanalization of the thrombosed veins.
