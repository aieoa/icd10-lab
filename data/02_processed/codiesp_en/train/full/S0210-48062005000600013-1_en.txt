A 69-year-old woman with a personal history of unrelated chronic renal failure (CKD), rheumatoid arthritis (RA) of 20 years' duration, on chronic treatment with corticosteroids. The patient began a sudden episode of total anemizing hematuria, hemodynamic compromise, accompanied by significant deterioration in renal function (creatinine 6.9); after orthotic knee surgery. The urological ultrasound of the kidneys is normal, with no alterations in the parenchyma or collecting system; A large bladder clot is observed.
After failure of conservative management with washing solution, the patient presented hemodynamic destabilization, requiring endoscopic exploration, making it impossible to extract the clot; Given the situation, it was decided to open surgery, open the bladder, extract the clot, and see a smeared bladder mucosa with bleeding in sheets, with no macroscopic bladder lesions seen. Random biopsies of the bladder mucosa were taken and were reported as Bladder Amyloidosis with extensive vessel involvement. Treatment is proceeded with corticosteroid infusion and colchicine. The patient was discharged without hematuria and improvement in renal function (creatinine 2.3).
