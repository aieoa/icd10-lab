We present a 21-year-old homosexual man with no relevant pathological history, who is referred to the digestive clinic due to "anal pruritus of 9 months' duration resistant to usual treatment." At that time, his doctor had prescribed local treatment with various corticosteroid creams and hygienic-dietary measures without improvement. Anal examination revealed the presence of a pigmented lesion, with slightly warty edges, in the posterior perianal region. Rectal examination and anoscopy were normal.

A skin biopsy was performed, the histological study of which reported the presence of hyperkeratosis and parakeratosis with altered cell maturation, affecting the entire thickness of the dermis. All of this is compatible with Bowenoid papillomatosis (grade 1 anal intraepithelial neoplasia). Antibodies against human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) were negative and other immunodeficiencies were ruled out.
