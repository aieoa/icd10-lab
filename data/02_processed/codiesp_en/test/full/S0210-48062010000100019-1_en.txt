53-year-old male patient, diagnosed with ankylopoietic spondylitis (HLA B27), secondary amyloidosis and end-stage chronic renal failure undergoing replacement therapy with hemodialysis. He presented with painless hematuria and the cystoscopy revealed a bladder mass, excrescent but not papillary and bleeding. A transurethral resection (TUR) was performed, with the histological diagnosis being bladder amyloidosis. The patient progressed satisfactorily and at 11 months he was readmitted with intense abdominal, epigastric and fixed pain, to which was added two hours later a sudden, abundant and bloody vomiting, which was complicated by cardiorespiratory arrest, which led to death (11 /04/1995). In the post-mortem anatomopathological diagnosis, ischemic necrosis of the territory supplied by the superior mesenteric artery and amyloid deposit in submucosal vessels were revealed in the digestive system. Organ and system involvement was widespread, including bilateral renal atrophy due to amyloidosis. Bladder amyloidosis (AA), especially perivascular and amyloidosis in the prostatic vessels.
