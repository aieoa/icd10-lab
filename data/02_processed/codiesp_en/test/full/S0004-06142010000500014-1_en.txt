A 48-year-old man from Argentina with no relevant personal history presented with enlargement and progressive pain in the left testicle for 2 months; He does not report urinary symptoms, nor fever. Treated with different antibiotic regimens without improvement.
On physical examination, a hard tumor was seen in the left testis, which was painless and without signs of fluctuation. Complementary tests: Blood count: Lymphocytosis is notable, the rest is normal. Urine sediment: Pyuria. Urine culture: sterile. Semen cultures: E. coli (1st), S. Sprophiticus (2nd), P. Mirabilis (3rd). Tumor markers: normal CEA, alpha fetus and BHCG. Urinary ultrasound: normal. Testicular ultrasound: Heterogeneous left testicular tumor without liquid areas inside. Mantoux positive with ulceration. In the absence of clinical-radiological improvement, radical orchiectomy was performed and the pathological study of non-specific orchitis was reported.

A sample is sent to microbiology, consisting of purulent exudate obtained intraoperatively from the testicular tissue. Auramine staining for mycobacteria was negative. In the Gram stain, polymorphonuclear leukocytes and few Gram(+) bacilli were observed, some of them filamentous, with irregular staining and with bulging club-shaped bacillary ends. Genomic amplification (PCR) for M.tuberculosis was negative. Chlamydia antigen detection was also negative. Culture for common bacteria, including Neiserias and Haemophilus, were negative after 72 hours of incubation.
At 96 hours, the growth of a microorganism that was very adherent to the medium, difficult to emulsify, was detected in the culture, which on Gram stain showed the morphology of a Gram(+) bacillus with a coryneform appearance, sometimes with irregular staining and some with club-shaped endings Modified Ziehl Neelsen stain was negative.
The phenotypic identification did not provide significant data, so the genetic study was carried out through 16SrRNA sequencing, identifying the strain as Corynebacterium thomsseni. Cultures for Nocardia, Mycobacteria and fungi were negative. The isolated strain was very sensitive to Penicillin, Ampicillin, Amoxicillin with clavulanic acid, Cefotaxime, Gentamicin, Amikacin, Erythromycin, Tetracycline, Trimethoprim sulfamethoxazole, Rifampicin and Imipenem.
