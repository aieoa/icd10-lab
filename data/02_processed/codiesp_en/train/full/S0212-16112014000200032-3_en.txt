A 73-year-old man diagnosed with infiltrating squamous cell carcinoma of the oropharynx G1, cT4b cN2, treated with chemotherapy and radiotherapy until 3 months ago.
He presented dysphagia secondary to radiotherapy to both solids and liquids, with a weight loss of 10 kilograms (kg) in two months. He received enteral nutrition via NGT for two years, and was eventually scheduled for PEG. During the oral endoscopy, bleeding occurred when dilating a stenosis in the upper esophageal portion, so the procedure was discontinued and PRG was performed, which was performed without incident.
