A 52-year-old woman with seropositive erosive RA, high titers of rheumatoid factor and anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide (anti-CCP) antibodies for 12 years, with secondary failure to infliximab and adalimumab. She presents with red eyes, pain and photophobia. Examination: crescentic peripheral corneal infiltrate associated with scleritis. Negative corneal scraping culture. We started treatment with oral prednisone 1 mg/kg/day and decided to change the therapeutic target to rituximab at the usual dose in RA with methotrexate 25 mg/week. After the first cycle of rituximab, the patient experienced great improvement with healing of the PUC and entered into clinical remission of the RA. After 10 months, a corneal infiltrate and mild scleritis and onset of joint symptoms appeared again. We decided to maintain treatment with rituximab i.v. on RA regimen every 8 months with good clinical control of both his ocular and systemic symptoms.
