Thirteen-year-old woman presents with a 12-day history of fever accompanied by left axillary lymphadenopathy. The initial examination was negative except for scratches on the left hand. Initial laboratory data included: leukocytes 4,700 (32% lymphocytes; 9% monocytes and 51% granulocytes); ESR 14 mm/h; CRP 2.4 mg/dl; negative blood culture. Given the persistence of fever and the presence of lymphadenopathy, Cat Scratch Disease was suspected, Bartonella henselae serology was performed and treatment with rifampicin was started, with the fever disappearing 3 days after starting treatment. The diagnosis was later confirmed when serology for Bartonella henselae was positive.
