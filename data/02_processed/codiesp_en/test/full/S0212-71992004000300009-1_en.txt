A 22-year-old man came to the clinic because of a painful adenopathy in the left laterocervical region for 4 months, which did not decrease in size with the use of antibiotics or NSAIDs. He reported having presented with several days of fever of up to 38 ºC and profuse night sweats, coinciding with cough and pharyngeal discomfort, symptoms that disappeared in a few days.
On examination, the patient had a 2 cm lymphadenopathy in the left laterocervical region. in diameter, not adhered to superficial or deep planes, not painful on palpation and of elastic consistency. He also had two other lymphadenopathy with similar characteristics, approximately 1.5 cm in diameter, in the left supraclavicular region and right laterocervical region. The rest of the examination did not present any findings of interest.
Complete blood count, biochemistry with tumor markers (CEA, α FP, PSA), chest X-ray, abdominal ultrasound and immunological study (ANAs, ANTI-RNP, ANTI-SSB, ANTI-Sm, ANTI-RO, ANTI-CENTROMERO, FR) were performed and immunoglobulins), the results being normal. The Mantoux was negative.
Serologies were performed, with the following results: toxoplasmosis, brucellosis, CMV, EBV, HIV, Syphilis, adenovirus, mycoplasma, HBV, HCV, Salmonella typhi and paratyphi, C. psitacci, pneumoniae and trachomatis and Rickettsia negative; immunized for rubella and measles (IgG +, IgM -); positive serology for Mumps (IgG + weak, IgM +).
A biopsy of the left laterocervical adenopathy was performed, with the following result: lymph node that shows its preserved architecture, except in an area that affects the cortical and paracortical in which a necrotic process associated with T lymphoid proliferation is observed, made up of very lymphocytes. activated, with the presence of immunoblasts and abundant histiocytes, many of which phagocytose cellular debris. No presence of granulocytes is observed. The rest of the lymph node shows follicles with activated germinal centers and stimulation of the paracortical area. This biopsy was diagnostic of histiocytic necrotizing lymphadenitis or Kikuchi-Fujimoto disease.
