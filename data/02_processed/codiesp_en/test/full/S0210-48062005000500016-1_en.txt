A 72-year-old patient was admitted for elective surgery for an infrarenal abdominal aortic and right hypogastric artery aneurysm. Her personal history highlights right nephrectomy in 1983 for renal cell carcinoma, HBP controlled with amlodipine, and old inferolateral AMI with silent heart disease. The DIVAS shows diffuse aortic ectasia and a fusiform aneurysmal dilation whose neck is located 17 mm from the left renal artery, with a 7 mm mural thrombus inside and a maximum lumen caliber of 35 mm at the level of the inferior mesenteric artery. The aneurysmal dilation affects both common iliac arteries, and a second 13 mm fusiform aneurysm is observed in the right hypogastric artery. Patency of the celiac axis, left renal artery, superior mesenteric artery and occlusion of the inferior mesenteric artery were confirmed.
The patient underwent surgery by placing a 36-12 aortic endoprosthesis (Quantum Cordis®) with extension to both iliac arteries and embolization of the right hypogastric artery. The control renal arteriography was normal.
Two hours after the intervention, sudden onset anuria was observed, without improvement with infusion of diuretics. Urgent arteriography (6 hours postoperatively) shows total occlusion of the ostium of the left renal artery due to cranial migration of the endoprosthesis. It was decided to undergo urgent revascularization surgery using a splenorenal shunt through a left posterolateral lumbotomy at the XIIth rib level. We proceed to dissection of the left renal pedicle (1 artery, 1 vein), renal reperfusion using lactated ringer's at 4 C and end-to-end anastomosis with the splenic artery (discontinuous Prolene® 6/0 sutures). Kidney reperfusion was carried out 12 hours after the onset of anuria, progressively recovering color and consistency, with the anastomosis maintaining a good pulse.
Spontaneous diuresis reappeared after 48 hours, previously requiring a hemodialysis session due to hyperkalemia and acidosis. The control Angio MRI (2 months) demonstrates the patency of the endoprosthesis, the absence of endoleaks and stenosis in the splenorenal anastomosis. Currently the patient continues to have well-controlled tensions with amlodipine and a creatinine level of 1 .4 mg/dl.
