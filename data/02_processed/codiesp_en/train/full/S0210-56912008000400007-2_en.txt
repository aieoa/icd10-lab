The patient was a 31-year-old man who suffered a motorcycle accident. The chest x-ray showed mediastinal widening. TACHT on admission showed a thoracic aortic pseudoaneurysm distal to the exit of the left subclavian artery with a significant periaortic and mediastinal hematoma associated with bilateral hemothorax.

He was admitted to the ICU, where strict blood pressure control was achieved. Eight hours after the trauma, an endoprosthesis was placed in the descending thoracic aorta via the left iliac route. The left subclavian artery was partially occluded without clinical repercussions. After 24 hours he was discharged to the ward and a week later he was discharged from the hospital, since he was asymptomatic.
