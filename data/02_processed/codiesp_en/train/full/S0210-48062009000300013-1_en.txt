66 year old male
Personal history: Prostatic adenomectomy, left renal colic, peptic ulcer. No previous hypertension. Heterozygous betathalassemia. Uric pyelic lithiasis of the left kidney.
Carrier of double J catheter at the time of ESWL.
Leoch ineffective 4 weeks before the second, in which the hematoma occurred. (4,000 impacts at intensity 4 and 1,000 at 5).
He had severe pain at the end of the procedure that required supplemental IV analgesia, although without hemodynamic alteration.
He was discharged after Leoch and was treated urgently after 5 days with poor general condition, asthenia, left lumbar pain and Hb of 8, with the hematoma being confirmed on echo and CT. Creatinine elevation up to 1.8.

Ten days after Leoch with Hb stabilized at 8 after administration of 3 concentrated red blood cells, and given the persistence of intense lumbar pain and fever (38-39º C), the hematoma was drained through a lumbar incision, verifying the existence of subcapsular hematoma, not infected, without active bleeding. After 48 hours, kidney function had normalized, fever had disappeared, and he only reported moderate pain typical of lumbotomies. She was discharged 4 days after the intervention. At 2 months, renal ultrasound, IVU, and renal function were normal.
