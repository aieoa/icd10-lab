A 41-year-old woman diagnosed with RRMS since she was 29. Given the lack of effectiveness of interferon-beta, it was decided to switch to natalizumab (EDSS 2), with no new outbreaks appearing during said treatment. This drug was suspended after two years due to positive JCV serology and, after a washout period (LP) of four months without any pharmacological treatment, it was decided to start fingolimod (EDSS 2). Two months after the onset and after radiological stability of the disease for more than two years, three new demyelinating lesions appeared on Brain Magnetic Resonance (CMR) without clinical evidence of a flare. From then on, the disease was radiologically stable until 28 months later, when the patient suffered a flare-up with an increase in the number of lesions in both semi-oval centers. Despite clinical worsening after resolution of the outbreak (EDSS 3), the patient continues on fingolimod (30 months from start to date). Regarding the safety of fingolimod, lymphopenia was evident from its beginning (300-900 cells/mmc), without leading to its withdrawal.
