51-year-old male. ACKD of unknown etiology, hypertensive and on hemodialysis since 2005, with first kidney transplant that immediately failed due to non-immunological arterial thrombosis in October 2007, receiving a transfusion of 2 units of red blood cells during said admission. Sporadic increase in transaminases in 2009, until becoming persistent since December 2010 (maximum level 28/40 IU/l GOT/GPT). Occult HCV was diagnosed at 14,700 copies/µg total RNA in PBMC (serum RNA by ultracentrifugation and high-sensitivity anti-HCV core antibody, negative). Normal ultrasound and FibroScan®. There is no external possibility of contagion, and PCR and viral serology were performed on the rest of the patients in the unit, as well as on health personnel, without detecting new cases of classic HCV infection. He received a second kidney transplant in October 2013, being treated with thymoglobulin (TG), tacrolimus (FK), mycophenolate (MMF) and corticosteroids. After 17 months after the transplant, the viral load in PBMC is undetectable, and the RNA by ultracentrifugation in serum and the highly sensitive anti-HCV core antibody remain negative.
