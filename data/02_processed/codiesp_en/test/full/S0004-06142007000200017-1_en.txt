29-year-old male patient. He was referred to our service to complete the study because, after the appearance of a painless tumor in his left testicle that had been going on for a month, the ultrasound detected a hypogenic left testicle that was reduced in size, and a right testicle with suspicious heterogeneous parenchyma. of neoplasia.
He has no other urological symptoms, no asthenia, no weight loss. He also has no personal history of interest.
On physical examination, a hypotrophic left testis was palpated, and a slightly enlarged right testis. Both are not painful, of normal consistency and regular surface. Epididymides and cords without pathological findings.
The analytical determinations highlight haemostasis, biochemistry and blood count within the parameters corresponding to normality, and an alpha-feto protein of 2.7ng/ml with a beta-hCG of 4.2mIU/ml.
During admission, a new ultrasound with Doppler flow was performed and the following was observed: a right testis measuring 49mm x 36mm x 26mm with a hypoechoic lesion, with lobulated edges, heterogeneous, occupying practically the entire testicle, and presenting increased Doppler flow. And a 32mm x 22mm x 20mm heterogeneous left testis with a slightly hypoechoic focal lesion of approximately 9mm with subtle Doppler flow. Additionally, bilateral testicular microlithiasis.
A chest X-ray was also performed, in which no pathological findings were noted, and a thoraco-abdomino-pelvic CT with contrast, where no lymphadenopathy or metastases were identified in any of the territories visualized.
With the diagnosis of bilateral testicular tumor, a bilateral inguinal orchiectomy was performed and hormone replacement treatment was prescribed.
In the pathological anatomy they report: atrophic left testicle with multiple microscopic foci of classic seminoma associated with large areas of intratubular germ cell neoplasia (ICGN) with absence of tumor infiltration in albuginea, rete testis, epididymis or vascular infiltration. The right testicle presents a tumor with characteristics of classic seminoma that occupies practically the entire parenchyma, associated with foci of intratubular germ cell neoplasia (ICGN) and foci of atrophy in the remaining testicle. Tunica albuginea, rete testis, epididymis and surgical edge free of tumor.
Treatment was completed with two cycles of chemotherapy with carboplatin according to hospital protocol.
He has currently been in complete remission of the disease for more than three years.
