4-year-old patient who comes to the clinic due to fatigue, the examination being normal; 5 days later, she has an urticarial rash on her lower extremities that has disappeared when she arrives at the clinic. Two days after the last visit, a fever of 38.5 °C appears, it seems that she has a cold and the fatigue has become more pronounced, with the macular rash persisting that now affects the lower and upper extremities and face, although we cannot see it on the skin either. the time of exploration.
Physical examination: periorbital and eyelid edema more pronounced in the morning of the last 3 days. Mouth breathing with nasal obstruction, striking submaxillary and laterocervical lymphadenopathy, congestive tonsils, and splenomegaly and hepatomegaly of 4 cm on abdominal palpation. Striking inguinal lymphadenopathy 2 cm in diameter. Bloated facies. There is no rash. Normal neurological. Axillary temperature of 38 oC. The rest of the examination and cardiopulmonary auscultation are normal with no signs of respiratory failure.
Analytical results
- Hemogram: red blood cells 4,000,000, hemoglobin 10.9 g/dl, hematocrit 31.6%, MCV 78.4. Leukocytes: 21,200 (neutrophils 2%, segmented 31%, lymphocytes 50%, monocytes 5%, reactive lymphocytes 9%, lymphoplasmic cells 3%); platelets 203,400/ml.
- Transaminases: AST 245 and ALT 271 (mU/ml).
- Paul Bunell: negative.
- Negative urine analysis.
On the first visit, a chest and abdominal x-ray was performed: alveolar condensation that affects LSD and corresponds to pneumonia; possible hilar lymphadenopathy; abdomen with moderate splenomegaly.
Four days later it was repeated: positive agglutination test (infectious mononucleosis). Hb 10.6; Htct 31.1; MCV 78.9; leukocytes 15,400 (segmented 27%, lymphocytes 60%, monocytes 5%, reactive lymphocytes 8%); platelets 246,800/ml. GOT/GPT 86/114 (mU/ml). The chest x-ray has improved compared to the previous control (residual condensation in LSD and LMD).
Investigations pending first visit: pharyngotonsillar exudate: normal flora in aerobic culture; Epstein Barr detection: EBV Ac antianticapsid IgM 36.3 (positive greater than 11.5), EBV Ac IgG 51.2 (positive greater than 11.5).
Chest x-ray 10 days after the 1st visit: increased density in LSD of paramediastinal distribution associated with displacement of the minor fissure, suggesting segmental atelectasis probably residual to the infectious process.
Diagnosis: infectious mononucleosis with pneumonia2-6.
