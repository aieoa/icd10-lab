A 42-year-old man was referred to our hospital due to recurrent episodes of conjunctivitis and episcleritis that did not improve with conventional treatment.
He did not report any personal or ophthalmological history of interest, only a smoking habit of approximately one pack a day.
During the ophthalmological examination, the corrected visual acuity (VA) was unity in both eyes; Biomicroscopic findings included conjunctival injection and chemosis +/+++, upper eyelid edema, and lower superficial punctate keratitis. He did not have eyelid retraction or exophthalmos and the intraocular pressure (IOP) was 13/11 mmHg. Classification according to the NONSPECS index of 2-b, 5-a.
Basic blood tests (biochemistry and blood count) were normal, thyroid hormones (T3, T4, TSH) were elevated. The patient was referred to the endocrinologist with the diagnosis of mild-moderate GO in the active phase, who instituted antithyroid treatment along with topical ophthalmological ocular treatment.
In subsequent reviews, the progression of ophthalmopathy towards a moderate-severe stage was noted with worsening of biomicroscopic signs, development of upper eyelid retraction of 3 mm in both eyes and lower eyelid retraction of 2 mm in the right eye (OD) and 1 mm in the right eye. left (LE), Hertel exophthalmometry of 25 mm in the OD and 24 mm in the LE and increased IOP (24/22 mmHg). An octreoscan was performed, which consists of an injection of 6 mg of intravenous In111-octreotide and planar imaging with photon emission tomography study at four and twenty-four hours. These images revealed the presence of activity at the orbital level using a qualitative contrast method comparing the uptake with that of the background (occipital bone tissue), therefore, treatment was started with lanreotide 90 mg every 3 weeks for 2 months, followed by 60 mg intramuscularly every 15 days for 2 more months.

After treatment, the test was repeated, confirming a decrease in orbital uptake. The corrected VA was 1 in the RE and 0.9 in the LE, with mild inferior corneal epitheliopathy persisting. The IOP was 17/14 mm Hg with topical hypotensive treatment in the RE and trabeculectomy in the LE (one month after starting treatment) due to sustained hypertension (≈36 mmHg) without response to medical treatment. Exophthalmometry of 22 mm AO and upper eyelid retraction of 2 mm and lower eyelid retraction of 1 mm AO while awaiting surgical correction.
