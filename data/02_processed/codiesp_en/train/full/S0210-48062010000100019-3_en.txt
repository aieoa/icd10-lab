An 84-year-old female patient diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis, who was admitted urgently due to abdominal pain and distention. With the diagnosis of subocclusive ileus, medical treatment was initially instituted. Without any urological history, a few hours after urethral catheterization she began to experience hematuria, which became very intense, evolving to the formation of a large bladder clot that occupied the entire cavity, and perivesical fluid and gas were also observed on the computed axial tomography. An urgent surgical intervention was performed, observing a section of ileum 1.5 m long up to 10 cm from the ileocecal valve, with an edematous violaceous appearance, which recovered acquiring a normal appearance, coloration and peristalsis; He had a small fissure in the bladder wall, so a cystotomy was performed to drain the large clot, observing bleeding in the wall sheet and great friability, taking multiple biopsies and subsequently performing ligation of both hypogastrics. . In the immediate postoperative period, after experiencing anuria and severe hemodynamic alteration, she died (05/09/2000). The pathological diagnosis revealed that all bladder biopsies that were performed had vascular and interstitial amyloid (AA) deposits.
