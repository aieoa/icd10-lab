Female patient who at birth presented with a bluish spot in the right nasoorbital region, which grew rapidly during the following month.
When she was seen by us at the age of 6 months, she had a 4 cm pedunculated mass that affected the nasoorbital groove and nasal dorsum. The girl had acquired the habit of looking at the lesion and presented strabismus.

Five days later she began treatment with intralesional injections of Betametazone 4 mg. After 4 sessions at four-week intervals, the fibrosed hemangioma was excised, using a supraciliary incision.

The postoperative evolution was satisfactory.
