8-year-old girl when she suffers a sprained left ankle. She is diagnosed with CRPS of the left ankle and foot, which she completely resolves in a couple of weeks with medical treatment.
Two months later, and without any previous trauma, she developed pain in her right ankle and foot, which was also diagnosed as CRPS. She was referred to the Pain Unit by the Pediatric Traumatology Service two months after this diagnosis.
Treatment was started with pregabalin plus tramadol, plus the application of the 8% capsaicin patch due to the severe allodynia she presented, without obtaining adequate relief from the pain or the rest of the accompanying symptoms.
For this reason, it was decided (with prior parental consent) to implant, in the operating room and under general anesthesia, a lumbar epidural catheter and connect it to an external pump for the administration of a continuous infusion of bupivacaine for two weeks; After that time, the infusion is stopped and the catheter is removed. Complete remission of symptoms that she continues.
