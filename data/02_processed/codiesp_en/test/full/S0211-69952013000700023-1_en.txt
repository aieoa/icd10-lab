A 46-year-old patient with no relevant history came to the emergency room due to pain in the left testis radiating to the ipsilateral flank, without fever or accompanying voiding syndrome. Unremarkable physical examination, except for arterial hypertension. Renal and testicular-prostatic ultrasound without findings. Rapidly progressive deterioration of renal function is observed (creatinine 9.79 mg/dl, 4.85 g/24 proteinuria without active sediment) and progressive anemization (Hb 8.1 g/dl, mean corpuscular volume 88 fl, mean corpuscular hemoglobin 31.1, mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration 35.4). Immunological study (antinuclear antibodies, antineutrophil cytoplasmic antibodies, anti-GBM, antistreptolysin, rheumatoid factor, C3-4), viral serology and tumor markers were normal. Proteinogram-immunofixation with monoclonal immunoglobulin (Ig)A-kappa band. IgG 317, IgA 1446, IgM 15 mg/dl, free light chain (FLC, Free-Lite® nephelometry) kappa 4090 ng/ml, lambda 1. Uric acid 10.8, LDH 269, calcium 10.2, albumin 3, 3, B2 microglobulin 23340. Liver and lipid profile and rest of blood count, normal. Nephropathy due to kappa light chain casts (CLL-K) and IgA-kappa MM IgA-kappa stage IIIB of Durie Salmon were anatomopathologically diagnosed, and treatment was initiated with bortezomib-dexamethasone, along with renal purification therapy with a Theralite® high cut-off filter every other day. .

During the following two months, the serum IgA level decreases progressively and significantly, but not the pre-dialysis CLL-K level, but it is decided in a multidisciplinary clinical session to continue with the treatment established according to the Hematology protocol and rebiopsy the kidney to assess the continuation of purifying therapy. In the rebiopsy, chronic tubulointerstitial lesions of mild-moderate intensity were observed, and 6 more cycles of high cut-off dialysis were prescribed.

Three months after diagnosis, after 4 cycles of chemotherapy, bortezomib was declared ineffective and was replaced by lenalidomide adjusted to renal function. Due to economic reasons, continuation of CLL purification therapy is impossible (having received a total of 18 sessions), and the patient remains on renal replacement therapy with conventional high-flow dialysis. The effectiveness of lenalidomide (Task Force, SWOG and EMBT) is confirmed, on which the patient is under three years after diagnosis, in addition to renal replacement treatment.
