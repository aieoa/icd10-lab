A 67-year-old woman with a personal history of appendectomy, herniated disc and hiatus, fracture of the tibia and fibula, as well as right renal colic, attended the urology clinic for a check-up. In the ultrasound study, a 5 cm solid neoformation was observed in the right kidney; A CT scan was performed, which described a mass at the cortical level of the right kidney, measuring approximately 5 cm, with lobulated external edges, which did not appear to affect the perirenal space, with no lymphadenopathy or dilation of the renal excretory duct observed. With the diagnosis of probable hypernephroma, right radical nephrectomy was performed by laparoscopy.

The macroscopic study describes a cortical neoformation with a maximum diameter of 5 cm, well delimited, but not encapsulated, which deforms the cortical surface without crossing it, with a yellowish cut surface with hemorrhagic-looking areas and cystic areas.
In the microscopic study, the neoformation is made up of acinar structures covered by small uniform cuboidal cells, with little cytoplasm and nuclei with uniform chromatin, immersed in a slightly cellular stroma. An immunohistochemical study was performed to determine vimentin, cytokeratin 7, broad-spectrum cytokeratin, CD34, chromogranin and EMA (Master Diagnostic. Granada. Spain), observing generalized expression of vimentin, focal expression of cytokerain 7 and EMA in the epithelial cells, and CD34. in the stromal component.

The patient's progress after the intervention was without notable incidents and she is asymptomatic 24 months after the intervention.
