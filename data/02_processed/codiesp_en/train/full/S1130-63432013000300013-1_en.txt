57-year-old male, unremarkable history and smoker 10-20 cigarettes/day. The patient presented chronic hepatitis C genotype 1a/1c, viral load 1,662,178 IU/ml and a degree of fibrosis F2-F3 (Fibroscan 9.9 Kilopascals) with an IL-28 CT polymorphism. It was decided to start his first treatment for hepatitis C with RBV 400mg/12h, P-INF alfa 2a 180mcg/week and Telaprevir 750mg/8h (with meals). At the beginning of treatment, the patient had a hemoglobin (Hb) of 157 g/L, a neutrophil count of 4.2x109 no/L and 1 58x109 no/L. Liver enzymes were slightly increased, GPT 114 IU/L, ALAT 115 IU/L and ASAT 80 IU/L.
In week 4, the patient had an undetectable viral load, Hb 136 g/L, neutrophil count 2.0 x 109 no/L and platelets 110 x 109 no/L. He reported anorectal discomfort, specifically hemorrhoids and anal itching, slight anxiety that improved with Lorazepam, and mild body itching that improved with the use of emollient creams.
In week 8 of treatment he was admitted to the hospital due to epigastric pain that became more intense and radiated to both hypochondria during the last 36 hours. He did not have fever, chills, nausea, vomiting or coluria. Analyzes were performed, which showed an amylase of 1888 IU/L, Hb 107 g/L, neutrophil count 2.7 x 109 no/L, and platelets 97x109 no/L. An absolute diet was established (except for taking Telaprevir, with whole milk) and analgesic and antiemetic measures. The patient progressed favorably and was discharged after one week of admission with an amylase of 173 IU/L.
The next day he was admitted again for intense epigastric pain radiating to both hypochondria and an amylase of 3406IU/L. Acute pancreatitis was diagnosed. Given the recurrent course (two admissions), Telaprevir was suspended (week 9 of treatment) due to the possibility that it was the cause and RBV and P-INF were continued. The patient was discharged five days later with an amylase of 365 IU/L.
At week 12 after the start of treatment, the viral load was undetectable, Hb 109 g/L, neutrophil count 1.1 x 109 no/L and platelets 1 58x109 no/L. He will continue treatment without Telaprevir until week 24.
