A 56-year-old woman presented with mild and inconstant pain in the upper right hemiarch for thirteen months, but it has worsened in the last week, making the patient incorrectly think that its origin was in the upper right molar. On inspection, only a mesio-distal crack was seen in the crown of the upper right 1st premolar.

In the radiographic examination we found nothing. The emergency treatment was: cameral opening of said premolar and placement of a medication inside (Cresophene, Septodont) provisionally covered with a cement (Cavit, Espe). The toothache disappeared and later it was planned to do a root canal and place a protective metal-porcelain crown.
