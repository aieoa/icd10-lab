60-year-old patient diagnosed with multiple high-grade papillary urothelial carcinoma (T1 G2-3) treated by bladder TUR on 01/12/05 and subsequent TUR due to probable recurrence on 05/09/05 (PA: chronic inflammatory tissue) to which Complementary treatment was indicated with BCG instillations, a weekly dose. For six weeks, he came to our center due to a constitutional condition of deterioration in his general condition with marked asthenia, fever of up to 39.5 ºC, predominantly in the evening, and joint pain that began in the right foot. , with posterior involvement of the left foot, ankles, wrists, hands and fingers (symmetrical and additive involvement) along with conjunctivitis with marked ocular redness and purulent discharge. No thrush or other oral or genital lesions reported. Without other symptoms or signs of infectious locality.
In the analysis carried out, he showed marked leukocytosis (22,180 with 80% neutrophils), ESR 100 at the 2nd hour and CRP 49 mg/dl. Added to this was alteration of liver enzymes.
Serology for hepatitis negative, as well as for Yersinia and Chlamydia. Both blood cultures, urine cultures and stool cultures were negative. Culture of Koch bacillus in urine with negative Kinyoun stain. HLA B27 and RF negative.
Subsequently, right ankle arthrocentesis was performed with fluid with inflammatory characteristics. Cultivation and negative crystals.
Given the negativity of the tests performed and the arthrocentesis, as a differential diagnosis, we considered polyarthritis secondary to BCG instillations.
We started treatment with indomethacin and corticosteroids (methylprednisolone 40 mg) with good clinical and analytical response. Upon discharge, the patient continued treatment with indomethacin 75 mg (1-0-1) for 21 days.
The patient came for a check-up after three months, being asymptomatic of his illness.
