A 68-year-old female patient, who went to the pain clinic referred by her family doctor, was diagnosed with severe spondyloarthrosis, especially at the expense of the lumbar spine. She has been prescribed generic transdermal fentanyl at 25 μg/h by her doctor for 2 weeks, with regular pain control (VAS, 7-8). After studying the patient, the exchange of generic fentanyl for Durogesic® Matrix 25 is considered. μg/h. After 2 weeks, the patient presented better pain control (VAS, 3-4), requiring 1 g of paracetamol alone as rescue. After 3 months she continues with Durogesic® Matrix 25 μg/h, with acceptable pain control (VAS, 3), and sporadically, as a rescue, paracetamol 1 g.
