Female patient, age 38 years, diagnosed with celiac disease with 46 U antitransglutaminase antibodies (v.n. up to 5) and duodenal biopsy compatible with celiac disease. She subsequently suffered two spontaneous abortions and a transient cerebral ischemic attack. She is currently in the sixth month of pregnancy with anticardiolipin IgM antibodies 75 MPL/ml (v.n. up to 20) and anticardiolipin IgG in normal values. Hematological study did not show other prothrombotic alterations. She continues treatment with subcutaneous heparin and a gluten-free diet.
