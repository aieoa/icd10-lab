57-year-old woman, personal history of depression, undergoing medical treatment, operated on for varicose veins.
Under study by his primary care physician for low back pain of 1 year's duration, under medical treatment with NSAIDs. An abdominal ultrasound was performed and a mixed type cyst (solid and liquid) was found in the right kidney measuring 7.4x4.2 cm. in diameter with suspicion of hydatid cyst.
The patient is referred to our consultations. The examination is unremarkable and laboratory tests, including hydatid serology, are normal.
Abdominal CT was requested to complete the study, finding a tumor in the upper right interpolar and polar region with well-defined contour, internal septation and low content density that has a size of 70x45x40mm corresponding to a complex cyst, and no regional lymphadenopathy is seen. The characteristics of the cystic tumor do not allow us to completely rule out malignancy.

We proceed to perform a right nephrectomy and anatomopathological study of the specimen reporting: cystic formation of 4.5 cm. multiloculated and serous content, well delimited and covered with squamous epithelium, with a diagnosis of multicystic nephroma.
