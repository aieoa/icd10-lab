This is a 27-year-old man who was admitted for multiple trauma after falling from 15 meters. He is treated at the scene of the accident with a 6-point Glasgow Coma Scale and hemodynamic stability is achieved. The TACHT diagnosed a thoracic aortic pseudoaneurysm in the isthmus and accompanying periaortic hematoma. The associated injuries of all cases are described in Table 1.
The patient was treated with labetalol for strict control of blood pressure. The ECOT at 24 hours and one week of follow-up of the aortic injury did not show changes. On the ninth day he was discharged from the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and on the twenty-eighth day of the accident, an endoprosthesis was placed in the descending thoracic aorta through the left common iliac route with asymptomatic occlusion of the left subclavian artery.
