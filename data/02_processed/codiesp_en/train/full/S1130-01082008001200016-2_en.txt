A 28-year-old woman reported epigastric pain and constitutional syndrome for one month. CT showed a 15 x 13 x 7 cm mass dependent on the ascending colon with bilobar liver metastases; A right hemicolectomy with liver biopsy was performed. The pathological anatomy showed a tumor formed by spindle cells with great mitotic activity that, after ultrastructural examination and IHC, was diagnostic of GANT. Adjuvant treatment was performed with imatinib 400 mg orally daily. The follow-up CT scan at six months showed a partial reduction in liver metastases, a complete remission at one year, and after three years there is no evidence of local or distant recurrence.
