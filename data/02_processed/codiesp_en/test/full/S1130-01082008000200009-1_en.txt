Female patient, age 26 years, diagnosed with celiac disease with antitransglutaminase antibodies 73 U (normal value up to 10) and duodenal biopsy with enteropathy with severe villocyte atrophy (grade III). She presented good progress with a gluten-free diet, disappearing diarrhea and improving nutritional status. Six months later the patient became pregnant, presenting fetal death at 36 weeks. The following year she presents another pregnancy. Anticardiolipin IgG antibodies are determined: 20 GPL U/ml (v.n. < 11) and anticardiolipin IgM: 9 MPL U/ml (v.n. < 10). The hematological study did not show other prothrombotic alterations. She is medicated with acetylsalicylic acid at a dose of 200 mg/day, presenting a normal pregnancy and birth by cesarean section.
