A 36-year-old emmetropic woman came to our emergency department due to blurred vision without a previous flu episode in January 1992. On examination, she had visual acuity (VA) in the right eye (OD) of 20/30 and in the left eye ( OI) 20/60, cells in the anterior chamber of 1+ both eyes (AO) and multiple white dots of 100-200 microns more numerous in the area temporal to the macula in AO. Angiofluoresceinography (AFG) showed multiple hyperfluorescent spots. Treatment was started with 40 mg per day of methylprednisolone IM (Urbason® soluble 40 mg, Aventis Pharma S.A., Madrid), topical atropine sulfate 10 mg/ml (Atropina® 1%, Alcon Cusí S.A., Barcelona) every 12 hours and dexamethasone 1 mg/ml topical (Maxidex®, Alcon Cusí S.A., Barcelona) every 6 hours. After two weeks of treatment she achieved a VA of 20/20 in AO. The systemic tests requested (basic analysis, chest x-ray, luetic serology, angiotensin converting enzyme and Mantoux) were negative.

Four years later she presented a new recurrence, more pronounced in RE, with visual loss at the level of 20/60 RE and 20/30 LE, without evidence of field alterations. The same systemic regimen was started as in the previous episode with complete visual recovery after a month of treatment, with some white spots and superior temporal peripapillary pigment persisting in the RE.
In September 2001 she had a new, milder outbreak. The VA was 20/25 OD and 20/20 OI, with funduscopic examination showing yellowish-white spots distributed along the posterior pole and alteration of the macular retinal pigment epithelium (RPE) AO. Topical and systemic corticosteroid treatment was started as in the first episode. Three weeks after the start of the outbreak, VA decreased to 20/40 OD and 20/60 OI, so it was decided to add oral methotrexate (Methotrexato Lederle®, Whyeth, Madrid) at low doses. An HLA A29 study was requested, which was negative.
In April 2005, the patient remained asymptomatic, with VA in 20/30 RE and 20/25 LE. In the fundus, she had a lifting of the nasal juxtafoveolar neuroepithelium in the RE, the optical coherence tomography confirmed the suspected diagnosis of a MNVSR. The patient rejected the treatment, although five months later, with a VA in OD of 20/60, treatment for MNVSR was started, with photodynamic therapy (Visudyne®, Novartis Farmacéutica S.A., Barcelona) and intravitreal triamcinolone acetonide 4 mg in 0.1 ml (Trigón depot® 40 mg/ml, Bristol-Myers Squibb, Madrid). VA in the RE improved to 20/40".

At the current time, the patient has a VA of 20/40 RE and 20/25 LE, faint yellowish-white spots in the posterior pole of both eyes, AO peripapillary atrophy lesions, with involvement of the papillomacular bundle in the RE and several whitish lesions. inactive, inferior to the macula of the LE.
