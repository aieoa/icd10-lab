38-year-old patient diagnosed with ulcerative pancolitis since 2004, corticosteroid dependent. That same year he presented a picture of inflammatory bowel activity with bloodless diarrhea, significant impairment of his general condition and a weight loss of 15 kg in one month, as well as digestive intolerance to treatment with azathioprine (Imurel®). During the last 3 years he has required several admissions due to severe outbreaks of diarrhea (5-6 stools a day) with red blood.
In June 2007, he presented a prolonged outbreak of ulcerative colitis with moderate-severe activation and poor response to pharmacological treatment with cortyrefractory criteria, at which time cytopheresis began, receiving a session every 7 days for 5 weeks. The access routes were the antecubital veins of the arms, and no complications or adverse reactions occurred during the sessions. Since the second session he showed notable clinical improvement and is currently receiving oral treatment with mycophenolate-mofetil (CellCe®).
