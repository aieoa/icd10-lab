A 21-year-old woman, a carrier of the VHL gene, presents with a cerebellar hemangioblastoma diagnosed 2 years ago, when she went to the emergency room due to cardiorespiratory arrest after it ruptured. In the ophthalmological examination, a superior temporal HCR of ½ papillary diameter with a yellowish appearance is seen in the LE, with another satellite HCR, which, like a small juxtapapillary HCR, is not seen in the funduscopy but is seen in the angiography, giving a hyperfluorescent saccular image with the rest of the ophthalmological examination normal in AO as well as the OCT of the macula, with a VA of unity in both eyes.
