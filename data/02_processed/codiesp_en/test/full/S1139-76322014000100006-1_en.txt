Three-month-old boy presents with erythema and edema on the right side of the face, and the ipsilateral arm and hand.
He has had a lesion on the back of his right hand since he was born, which has intensified coinciding with generalized redness. It is a yellowish-brownish erythematous plaque measuring 25x18 mm in diameter, with a more indurated central area of ​​10x15 mm.

He is summoned to the Dermatology clinic for evaluation of a possible mast cell tumor, and there the diagnosis is confirmed and conservative treatment is recommended.
