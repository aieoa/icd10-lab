During his hospitalization, data of deep vein thrombosis in the left lower extremity verified by venous echo Doppler, severe pericardial effusion corroborated by chest X-ray, electrocardiogram, Doppler echocardiography and acrocyanosis in the distal phalanges of the fingers of both hands were added. Pulses were performed with 500 mg methylprednisolone for 3 consecutive days, continuing with prednisone at a dose of 1 mg/kg/day and azathioprine 100 mg/day, in addition to anticoagulation with heparin sodium and warfarin.
The evolution was unfavorable, with digital necrosis (dry gangrene), associated signs of psychosis, lupus nephropathy and cerebral ischemia (left hemiparesis), documented with brain CT and electroencephalogram. She was treated with 3 sessions of plasmapheresis weekly for two weeks, pulses with 1 g of cyclophosphamide weekly for 3 consecutive weeks and immunoglobulin 20 g per day (400 mg/kg/day) for 7 days, pentoxifylline, flunarizine, levothyroxine, antivirals and antihypertensives. She was discharged after 6 weeks of hospitalization with neurological sequelae and complete necrosis of the 1st and 2nd distal phalanges of the aforementioned fingers on both hands, which were amputated naturally 9 months after her discharge. She is currently undergoing periodic medical check-ups and treatment with mycophenolate mofetil 1 g/day with favorable evolution.
