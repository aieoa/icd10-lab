A 59-year-old woman with no relevant history presented with hematuria and pain in the right renal fossa and right hypochondrium for 6 hours. It is accompanied by nausea and hypotension with figures of 90/50. Analytically she presents leukocytosis with neutrophilia. An abdominal ultrasound was performed in which a heterogeneous image of 15 x 14.5 cm was seen depending on the right kidney. Several hours later her general condition worsened with a greater tendency to hypotension and an increase in abdominal mass. An abdominal CT scan was performed showing a large renal mass occupying the entire right hemiabdomen with the appearance of intratumoral bleeding and intra-abdominal free fluid. Surgical intervention was decided due to hemodynamic instability, performing right nephrectomy. The pathological study is reported as clear cell renal adenocarcinoma of 10 cm in diameter in the medial zone of the kidney that infiltrates the renal capsule without affecting the sinus or vascular and hilar structures; stage G II, T2.
