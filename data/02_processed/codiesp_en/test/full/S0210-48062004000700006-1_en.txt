60-year-old patient with a history of depressive disorders, with attempted self-harm, causing multiple trauma with fracture of the sacrum, right iliopubic branch (2 years of evolution); She subsequently suffered from repeated urinary infections, constipation and irritative pattern incontinence.
On physical examination, she presented atrogic external genitalia, a negative Boney test, and a discrete cystocele.
In the analysis, she presents a biochemistry with normal parameters and preserved kidney function. She highlights a sediment with leukocyturia and bactiuria.
The urine culture is contaminated. In the urographic study, she presented good bilateral function, with moderate right pyeloureteral ectasia, lack of bladder filling and bladder rejection.

Abdominopelvic scan: spleen, pancreatic area, kidneys with discrete right ureteral ectasia. Giant fecaloma that occupies practically the entire pelvis and produces great bladder displacement.

The Digestive Unit is consulted, where admission is made for manual extraction and evacuation enema therapy; proceed to cleanse the recto-sigma.
In subsequent check-ups, improvement was noted in his urination symptoms and control of constipation.
