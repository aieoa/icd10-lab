43-year-old man infected with HCV genotype 1a and human immunodeficiency virus (HIV), with grade F4 liver fibrosis (cirrhosis), FibroScan of 27 kPa and Child-Pugh grade A. Under treatment for HIV since February 2010 (tenofovir + emtricitabine + raltegravir).
The patient presented RP below normal levels (<120 x 109/L) since the diagnosis of HCC (December 2008). Before starting antiviral therapy for HCC, the patient had grade III thrombocytopenia (PR = 42 x 109/L), grade III neutropenia (Neutrophils = 0.66 x 109/L) and hemoglobin (Hb) values ​​within normal limits. (Hb = 14.1 g/dL). Alanine aminotransferase (ALT) and aspartate aminotransferase (AST) presented values ​​within normal limits (35 and 36 IU/mL, respectively) and gamma-glutamyltranspeptidase (GGT) was found to be slightly elevated (62 IU/mL). The HCV viral load (CVVHC) was 180,000 copies/mL and the CVHIV was undetectable.
In March 2012 he started treatment for HCC with pegIFN-α2a and RBV (180 mcg/week and 1,000 mg/day, respectively) for 11 weeks. In week 12, presenting PR of 26 x 109/L, TVR (750 mg/8 hours) was added. The PR progressively decreased to 16 x 109/L at week 14, at which time treatment with eltrombopag 50 mg/day was initiated and the dose of pegIFN-α2a was reduced to 135 mcg/week. In weeks 16 and 17, the PR continued below normal values ​​(14 x 109/L and 13 x 109/L, respectively). In week 22, the PR rose to 34 80 mcg/week), coinciding with the end of treatment with TVR. The dose of eltrombopag was reduced to 50 mg/day in week 30 and in week 35 it was suspended due to PR of 50 x 109/L. At the end of treatment (week 48), PR was 67 x 109 platelets/L with undetectable CVVHC (< 15 copies/mL) since week 15.
