A 61-year-old woman with a systemic history of obesity, high blood pressure, kidney failure with good control at present and type 2 diabetes mellitus of 7 years of evolution on insulin treatment for 2 years and in the last 3 months was associated with rosiglitiazone 4 mg per day. (Avandia®, Glaxosmithkline) which achieved good glycemic control. The ophthalmological history included diffuse bilateral macular edema treated with laser six months previously and with maximum vision of 0.3 in the right eye and 0.250 in the left eye. She goes to the ophthalmology service reporting severe vision loss that prevents her from carrying out her daily work for the last two months. In the systemic examination, she noted a weight gain of 7 kilos (data reported by her Primary Care doctor) with peripheral edema in the extremities. Visual acuity is 0.05 in both eyes. In the fundus, bilateral severe diffuse macular edema was observed. Due to generalized fluid retention, a systemic examination was performed, ruling out renal and/or cardiac pathology, establishing the association with rosiglitazone. She discontinued this drug and after two months the patient had lost 5kg and the edema in her legs had disappeared. The macular edema did not decrease and visual acuity at three months was still at 0.05 in both eyes.
