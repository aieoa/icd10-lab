A common case of dizziness, with an unusual diagnosis
An 11-year-old girl with no family or personal history of interest, who went urgently to the health center reporting, since the previous night, a sensation of movement of objects around her, with fear and anxiety. Physical examination is normal; otoscopy without alterations. Normal blood pressure and blood glucose. The absence of a vegetative component, instability, paleness or other symptoms such as tinnitus or tinnitus is striking. No neurological focality, balance or gait alterations, ataxia or other cerebellar signs were found. The patient had a history of taking Romilar® (dextromethorphan) the night before and a positive family history. With these data and a normal physical examination, she was sent to her home for observation, with an appointment for the consultation the next morning.
