This is a 40-year-old man who suffered a car accident. TACHT on admission showed a pseudoaneurysm of the thoracic aorta at the exit of the left subclavian artery with a periaortic hematoma extending to the posterior mediastinum. The patient was admitted to the ICU while breathing spontaneously with systolic blood pressure (SBP) less than 120 mmHg. Conservative treatment of the associated injuries was carried out.
Ten hours after admission to the ICU, a stent was placed in the descending thoracic aorta via the left femoral route, with partial occlusion of the left subclavian artery. He was discharged to the ward after 48 hours. He followed controls with TACHT at 7 days, at one month and at 45 days.

Two months after the accident he presented acute pericarditis and absence of left radial pulse without clinical repercussion. The TACHT showed correct location of the endoprosthesis.
