82-year-old patient, diagnosed after a study of normocytic-normochromic anemia by colonoscopy of right colon neoplasia. Both histological and cytological studies diagnosed MM. After an exhaustive extension study, which included thoracoabdominal CT, abdominal ultrasound, dermatological review (which included exeresis and study of two nevi that ruled out MM), fundus study and anorectal examination, the patient underwent surgery and underwent a right hemicolectomy.
The pathological study of the resection specimen confirmed the presence of a polypoid and ulcerated MM of 11 cm in diameter at the level of the ascending colon, which invaded the submucosa and focally the muscular layer. Numerous tumor cells contained melanin and the immunohistochemical study was positive for S-100 protein and HMB45. Of the 30 lymph nodes resected, 2 had metastases. The patient followed a correct postoperative course, being alive without evidence of disease 4.5 years after the intervention.
