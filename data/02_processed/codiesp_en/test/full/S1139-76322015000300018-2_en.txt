An 11-year-old girl presented with asthenia, weight loss and fever for 13 days, presenting fever peaks every day, without a fixed time pattern. On examination he had an asthenic habit (weight P3-10) and thick, whitish mucus in the cavum, with the rest of the examination normal.
In the analysis on admission: leukocytes 4060/µl (segmented 59%, cavities 6%, lymphocytes 33%, monocytes 2%), Hb 13.3 g/dl, Hct 38%, platelets 25,000/µl. In the smear, red blood cells in the helmet, activated lymphocytes and vacuolization of neutrophils are observed; CRP 58.1 mg/l (88.2 mg/l, in a visit to the emergency room two days before); proteinuria in the urine sediment. She was admitted with intravenous amoxicillin-clavulanic acid treatment due to the findings in the smear and the elevation of acute phase reactants. She remains afebrile since the second day of admission. During her admission, she presented micropetechiae on the lower limbs and dorsum of the feet on the second day, as well as bleeding from the oral mucosa on the third day; Given these findings and because on the fourth day an analytical control showed 8000 platelets/µl, it was decided to give a dose of gamma globulin, with an adequate response to treatment, presenting clinical improvement and an increase in the number of platelets up to 16,000/µl.

A study was carried out for fever without a long-term focus, with positive IgM parvovirus B19, making a diagnosis of parvovirus B19 infection, secondary plateletopenia and mild proteinuria, with the rest of normal kidney function.
