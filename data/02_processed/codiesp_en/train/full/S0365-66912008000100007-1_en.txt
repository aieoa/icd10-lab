Girl, 3 years and 8 months old. Gestational diabetes and Rh mother - grade A, 38 weeks gestation and normal delivery. Normal female karyotype 46 XX.
Prenatal ultrasound diagnosis of this girl shows agenesis of the corpus callosum and a multicystic dysplastic right kidney. In the central nervous system she presents a choroid plexus cyst and a cyst in the deep interhemispheric region. On magnetic resonance imaging examination, no abnormalities of neuronal migration are detected. Electroencephalographic (EEG) recordings were normal. Cognitive studies show a normal psychomotor development quotient.
Fundus examination shows a practically normal appearance of the right fundus, with a normal optic disc, two small hypopigmented chorioretinal lacunae, and no macular involvement. However, we observed in the fundus of the left eye a colobomatous malformation of the optic nerve and chorioretinal lacunae in greater numbers without macular involvement.
