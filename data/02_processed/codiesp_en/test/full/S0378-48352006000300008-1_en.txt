77-year-old male, with no relevant history. Consultation due to redness of the skin in the upper third of the chest and proximal part of both limbs along with proximal muscle weakness in the arms and legs, heliotrope erythema on the eyelids and violaceous papules on the knuckles of both hands, for one month. Analyzes were performed, highlighting creatine kinase 736 IU, LDH 545 IU, urea 63 mg/dl, CA 19.9 64.1 U/ml. Electromyogram: Findings compatible with myopathic inflammatory process. Muscle biopsy: Inflammatory changes compatible with dermatomyositis. With the diagnosis of dermatomyositis, treatment was started with prednisone 60 mg/day and study: Thoracoabdominal CT: 6 cm pancreatic mass and hypodense liver lesions. Liver puncture-biopsy: Adenocarcinoma. Referred to Oncology. After 8 weeks of corticosteroids, without improvement in the skin lesions or muscle weakness, and given the diagnosis of stage IVB pancreatic adenocarcinoma due to liver metastases, we began treatment with gemcitabine 1250 mg/m2 days 1 and 8 every 21. After 2 cycles of Treatment documents complete resolution of skin lesions, normalization of muscle enzymes and recovery of strength. Currently the patient remains asymptomatic after 6 cycles of treatment and with less than 50% response to his underlying disease.
