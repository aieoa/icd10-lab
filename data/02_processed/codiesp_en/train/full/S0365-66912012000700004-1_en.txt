A 38-year-old male, hypertensive, with sensorineural hearing loss, was referred for low visual acuity (VA). In the family history, three deaf-mute male brothers and another with low vision were recorded.
Uncorrected visual acuity (VA) was 0.3 in the right eye (RE) and 0.2 in the left eye (LE). An anterior lenticone was detected and the fundus revealed the existence of an AM in both eyes, surrounded by a perimacular ring of whitish dots. Optical coherence tomography (OCT) was performed with Stratus OCT-3 (Carl Zeiss Meditec, Dublin, CA), which verified the presence of AM, in stage IV, with a basal diameter of 1,347 μm in the RE and 1,409 in the LE, with hyperreflectivity in inner retinal layers and cystic changes in adjacent tissues. After a systemic study (including renal biopsy), AS was diagnosed, and the patient is currently on hemodialysis for severe renal failure, maintaining the characteristics of MA.
