A 33-year-old man, a smoker of a pack of cigarettes a day, with no other relevant history, came to the emergency room with penile induration at the base for ten days. The examination revealed an indurated longitudinal cord at the base of the penis located dorsally, mobile and painful. No inguinal lymphadenopathy was palpated. Feverish. A penile Doppler ultrasound was performed, which revealed an induration corresponding to segmental thrombosis of the superficial dorsal vein of the penis. Normal coagulation study.
Diagnostic Trial: Mondor Disease. The patient received anti-inflammatory treatment. He did not attend the review.
