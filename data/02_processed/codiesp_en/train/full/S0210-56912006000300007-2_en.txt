We present the case of a 49-year-old male patient; Four months before admission to our hospital, he underwent vascular surgery with aorto-biliac replacement for an infrarenal abdominal aortic aneurysm.
A week prior to admission to the Intensive Care Service, he had episodes of proctorrhagia whose volumes increased. After secondary hemodynamic decompensation, his admission to the Intensive Care Service was indicated. He also reported the presence of very intense periumbilical and hypogastric colicky pain, a hematocrit of 13% and uncontrollable bleeding, for which emergency abdominal surgery was indicated due to the suspicion of an AED, since the abdominal ultrasound performed hours before reported a superior linear image. to the umbilical scar compatible with a vascular prosthesis, with a hypoechoic area of ​​87 x 67 x 71 mm surrounding it. Exploratory laparotomy was performed, revealing retroperitoneal hematoma surrounding the previous prosthesis and FAE with jejunum 60 cm from the fixed loop and sigmoid colon. He presented cardiorespiratory arrest and died in the operating room.
