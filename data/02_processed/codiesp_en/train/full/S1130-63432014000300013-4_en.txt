A 75-year-old man, who had previously received complementary intravesical treatment with MMC and BCG. After tumor recurrence he started treatment with intravesical gemcitabine. During the first instillation, the patient experienced intense local burning, dizziness, and profuse sweating (grade 2),5 which forced treatment administration to be stopped. Although he presented hypotension (90/60 mmHg) postinstillation, the blood pressure levels normalized (130/80 mmHg) a few minutes later with postural measures. Dysuria improved during the subsequent 24 hours with oral anti-inflammatory treatment. The urine culture was negative. Treatment was suspended by patient decision. After two weeks he started intravesical treatment with MMC with good tolerance after four instillations.
