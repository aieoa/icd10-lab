A 7-year-old male presents with great facial asymmetry due to marked right facial hypoplasia. Using MRI we confirmed mandibular ankylosis accompanied by great secondary tissue retraction and microretrognathia, which only allows a maximum interincisal opening of 4 mm in addition to a class II malocclusion.
The ankylotic block of the right temporomandibular joint was surgically resected by osteotomy at the level of the condylar neck and right coronoidectomy, followed by reconstruction using a chondrocostal interposition graft. At the time of final discharge, the patient maintained a mouth opening of 3 cm.
