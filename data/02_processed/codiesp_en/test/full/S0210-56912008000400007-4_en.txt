The patient was a 35-year-old man who suffered a motorcycle accident. TACHT on admission showed a 7 mm intimal laceration in the thoracic aorta after exit from the brachiocephalic trunk.
The patient underwent an urgent decompressive right parietal craniotomy and evacuation of a subdural hematoma. Treatment with intravenous labetalol was initiated for strict control of SBP and the management of associated injuries was prioritized due to their severity. The ECOT after one week showed improvement compared to admission; at 15 days, the magnetic resonance angiography (MRI-angio) reported a normal distribution of the outflow of the supra-aortic trunks without images suggestive of traumatic rupture of the aorta. One month after admission he was discharged home.
