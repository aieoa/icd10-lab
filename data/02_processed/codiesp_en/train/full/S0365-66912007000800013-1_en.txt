25-year-old man with macular hemorrhage secondary to ocular trauma. His VA is 0.4 and his intraocular pressure is 15 mmHg. Because the size of the hemorrhage is not very large, it was decided to institute treatment with topical, oral and cycloplegic corticosteroids. The following week there was a decrease in VA to 0.1 and an increase in hemorrhage, so a posterior vitrectomy was performed. An attempt was made to displace the hemorrhage with perfluoropropane without success, and 0.1 ml of rt-PA was administered directly to the retina. a concentration of 50 µg/0.1 ml and the eyeball is filled with gas (30% SF6), positioning in the prone position for 1 week. Four weeks after the intervention, the VA is 0.8, the macular hemorrhage had disappeared, but once the blood was reabsorbed, the existence of a juxtafoveolar choroidal rupture was seen. At 6 months the VA is 1.0.
