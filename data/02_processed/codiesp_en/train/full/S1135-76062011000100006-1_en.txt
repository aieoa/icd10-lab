27-year-old man who receives a gunshot to the skull during a police chase. Transferred to a healthcare center, he died 24 hours later as a result of the brain damage. Among the autopsy findings, there were two holes in the scalp (right frontal and parietal) and a single bone entry hole in the right parietal bone without a bone exit hole. The cutaneous entry hole, without tattooing or smoking, is classified as "distant" or "long distance." After removing the scalp, an elongated bone entry hole is observed in the right parietal region, confirming external bevelling limited to the most posterior area of ​​the hole, with a fracture line. A fragment of the projectile was recovered in the right occipital region, deformed, made of bare lead, caliber .38, with a sector of its "mirror" surface. The rest of the projectile produces a cutaneous exit hole in the right parietal region, almost superimposed on the bony entry hole. Histological examination confirms the nature of the skin holes.
